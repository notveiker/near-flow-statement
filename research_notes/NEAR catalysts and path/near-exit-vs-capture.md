# 1Click is a net NEAR exit — fees don’t cancel the flow

*Exploratory. Twin: `data/derived/near_exit_vs_capture.json`.*

## Takeaway

In the measured window, 1Click sent about **$24.0M** out of NEAR against **$14.6M** in — net **~$9.4M** NEAR sold. Captured Intents revenue was **~$1.99M**. Net exit is about **4.7×** the period’s captured revenue. The buyback wallet (~**$8.0M** at $3.67) is similar in size to one window’s net exit, and it is **held, not burned**. Fee capture and NEAR-asset flow are different scoreboards; do not assume Intents “earns” NEAR scarcity just because volume is large.

## Punch

1. Venue volume can rise while NEAR is a net exit asset on the same pipe.
2. Captured revenue << net NEAR exit in the window — buybacks would need to scale or burn to matter for float.
3. Consistent with NEAR-on-one-side only ~3.5% of 1Click volume and inventory that is mostly non-NEAR (ZEC/stables/BTC).
