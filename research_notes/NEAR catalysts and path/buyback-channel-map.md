## Takeaway
**~45.8%** of explicit buyback fee dollars come from **no-referrer** flow (**18.7%** of volume at **10 bp**). LI.FI is **10%** of volume at only **2.8 bp**. Trust *direct* is **20 bp** on **2.4%** vol. SwapKit subchannels (Ledger/THORSwap/Trust/Tangem) carry high partner bp and ~0 explicit buyback. The attachment dial’s cash is concentrated in default/no-referrer — not in the headline wallet logos.

## Punch
1. Chase no-referrer and high-bp direct wallets for attach; don’t expect SwapKit logos to fund τ.
2. LI.FI is a volume pipe with thin NEAR line — second attach target after defaults.
3. Channel strategy ≠ peer volume strategy.
