# Intents vs L1 flow — deposits dwarf stablecoin transfers

*Exploratory. Twin: `data/derived/intents_vs_l1_flow.json`. Node census window (~10h).*

## Takeaway
Intents deposits ran about **$80M/day** in the census window against about **$13M/day** of measured stablecoin transfers — roughly **6×**. Intents is not only ~87% of NEAR fee attribution; it is the **dominant value-moving surface** on the L1 in that window. A window of hours is not a trend.

## Punch
1. Fee-share and flow-share both point at Intents as the investment object.
2. Base-layer stablecoin activity is second-order beside Intents settlement inventory turns.
3. Still requires capture discipline — flow without τ is vanity.
