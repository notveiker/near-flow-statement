# AI-agent settlement narrative vs evidenced usage

*Exploratory / post-freeze. As of 2026-11-11. Separates marketing stack from measured fee engine. Feeds no pre-registered test.*

## 1. What exists in the product stack

**Takeaway.** The AI / agent stack is real product surface (staking for inference, TEE tooling, x402 membership claims in public blogs) but is not yet a measured Intents fee line comparable to 1Click / Confidential Infrastructure Fee.

**Cited findings.**
- Third-party fact `ai_staking`: “More than 750,000 NEAR has already been staked for inference” (`data/raw/web/third_party_facts.json`).
- Bitwise NRR launch materials frame NEAR as an Intents / AI-agent proxy (capital-markets note); that is narrative packaging of the ETF, not a fee series.
- Roadmap items near-kit / TEE enforcement appear on roadmap.near.org timelines (catalyst calendar); product, not tokenomics.

**Inferences.** Agent accounts, confidential intents for agents, and inference staking are *enablers*. They do not appear as a DefiLlama revenue adapter line or as a named share of the three revenue wallets in the 30-day window.

**Gaps.** No public series for agent-originated Intents volume, NEAR-denominated agent payments, or x402 settlement counts in this repository as of 2026-11-11.

## 2. Quantitative evidence

**Takeaway.** The only hard number in-repo for the AI stack is **>750k NEAR** staked for inference — order **$2.8M** at $3.67, ~**0.06%** of mcap. That is demand for compute collateral, not fee capture.

**Cited findings.**
- `ai_staking` fact above.
- NRR ~$56M / 1.2% mcap (`data/raw/prop/etf_nrr.json`) — capital markets overlay sold partly on the AI story.
- Intents captured revenue ~$1.99M / 30d — the fee engine that actually moves issuance-offset math.

**Inferences.** AI staking is three orders of magnitude smaller than Intents inventory (~$229M) and two orders smaller than NRR AUM. Ranking by economic materiality today: Intents fees ≫ NRR ≫ AI inference stake.

**Gaps.** OpenRouter / IronClaw / x402 volume not in committed raw files; treat any press claim as marketing until hashed into `data/raw/`.

## 3. Interaction with the Intents fee thesis

**Takeaway.** Additive *story* for Bitwise / near.com marketing; substitute only if capital rotates into NRR on AI headlines without Intents capture improving. Not a substitute cash-flow.

**Cited findings.** Structural thesis: ETFs are a soft float overlay and a competing narrative, not a fee substitute. Catalyst path: score attachment / attestation / mix / HSP-029 before AI slides.

**Inferences.** AI narrative can raise the multiple investors will pay for the same Intents cash-flow; it does not raise captured bp by itself.

## 4. When agent settlement becomes first-order

**Takeaway.** First-order for the token when agent-originated volume shows up in public fee/volume series *or* NEAR-denominated agent payments are large vs issuance — neither is true yet.

**Falsifiers for “agents are the thesis.”** Inference stake stalls or unstakes; no agent line in revenue wallets after a disclosed agent GA window; x402 / IronClaw remain press releases without measurable settlement; Intents capture share falls while AI marketing rises (narrative decoupled from cash-flow).

## Proprietary punch

- Treat **>750k NEAR** inference stake as a call-option on a second demand surface, sized at ~0.06% of mcap — disclose it, do not lead with it.
- The investment-relevant AI interaction is whether Bitwise/NRR flows trade as an Intents/AI sentiment ticker while the cash-flow engine stays Intents attachment + confidential take.
- Until agent volume is in `data/raw/`, the honest scoreboard stays: attach %, capture share, escrow attestation, near.com mix, HSP-029.
