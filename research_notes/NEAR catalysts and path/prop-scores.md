## Volume-without-capture overstatement

Intents is **55.7%** of peer-set volume but only **32.5%** of its own gross fees reach NEAR wallets. Pricing the token as if volume leadership equalled cash-flow control overstates NEAR’s economic grip by roughly the capture gap (partners + solver economics + unattached routes). The scoreboard that matches the structural thesis is **token-claimable bp**, **capture share**, **attestation gap**, and **attachment %** — not peer volume share alone.

## How the scores move

- Attachment ↑ → token-claimable bp ↑ and distribution tax ↓ (if partner listed fees hold).
- Escrow attestation → attestation gap → 0 (collapses range; does not create revenue).
- near.com mix ↑ → capture share ↑ without raising user all-in on partner wallets.
- HSP-029 → same revenue offsets more of a smaller mint (coverage ↑ at constant capture).
