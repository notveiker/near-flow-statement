Can NEAR’s Intents business grow into its dilution?

NEAR Intents led its market over the past year. NEAR’s price asks for much more than that. The NEAR that Intents fees deliver to NEAR’s revenue wallets equals, at most, about a sixth of net new supply. For those fees alone to carry today’s price, they would have to grow faster each year to 2031 than cross-chain volume has grown over any period in the data. Owning NEAR at this price is a bet on a bigger cut of those fees, or on value beyond them. Read the case or see it as slides.

The questions and the pre-registered answer

The pre-registered question: how much NEAR actually flows to exchanges, and how much of that do identified buyers absorb? (It makes no claim about which coins are sold.) The question the evidence can answer: how much of NEAR’s value capture can you verify?

What the pre-registered tests found: net staking outflow was 6.56× staking rewards over 90 days, where H1a (most staking rewards stay staked) predicted under 0.5×. Only 20.0% of the top-100 NEAR receivers carry an exchange label, so how much NEAR reaches exchanges (H1b: less than is minted) and how much of it identified buyers absorb (H1c: at least 25%) come out as ranges too wide to settle either way. So the frozen rating rule gives no buy or avoid signal, which carries no view on NEAR.

Exploratory, chosen after the freeze: over 30 days the NEAR that verifiably reached NEAR’s three Intents revenue wallets was 15.81% of net issuance, of which 4.53 points trace to public fee collectors, 11.25 arrived as releases from an escrow whose fee stream is unattested, and 0.03 came from one other source. So, if those three are all of NEAR’s Intents revenue wallets, the value capture that can be verified is a range. This is not a valuation and not advice. See the value-capture ladder.

Notes and sources

NEAR delivered to NEAR’s three Intents revenue wallets equalled 15.81% of net issuance; only 4.53 points trace to public fee collectors and 11.25 arrive as unshields from the Confidential Intents escrow, fee stream unattested.

What it shows: How much of NEAR’s net issuance (88,295 NEAR a day over the 30 days 2026-10-06 to 2026-11-04) was matched by NEAR delivered to the three revenue wallets, split by how it arrived: 4.53% from two recurring public fee collectors and 11.25% released from the Confidential Intents escrow, plus 0.03% from one other source.

What it does not show: Which fee stream pays for the escrow releases: the NEAR verifiably arrived, but the public chain cannot split the 11.25 points between the two candidate NEAR-side streams that fit, so, if those three are all of NEAR’s Intents revenue wallets, measured value capture is a range, 4.53% to 15.81% of issuance.

  • The two candidate readings are near.com’s fees and the Confidential Infrastructure Fee. A per-stream attestation by NEAR would turn the range into one number. Nothing here alleges misconduct. This is consistent with a privacy feature working as documented, and the question is auditability.
  • The rungs are not nested: collectors, escrow and one other source add up to all three wallets, and the buyback-wallet rung is a subset of the total.
  • Not all of the collector rung is public settlement. Of its 4.53 points, 0.56 were fees paid by single-use accounts the escrow had funded (exploratory, every transfer into the collectors classified by sender), so 11.81 points passed through the escrow in all, and counting only publicly settled swaps the floor is 3.97%. By the last day, 96.0% of collector A’s fee value came from escrow-funded accounts.
  • The total matches DefiLlama: 418.8K NEAR is 100.9% of its reported Intents revenue for the same days. DefiLlama’s adapter takes the same three wallets from NEAR’s own Dune query, so it is the same count: it checks our pricing and wallet list and gives no independent check of the wallets. Even the top of the range covers about a sixth of issuance, and the buyback wallet holds its NEAR: none of it has been burned.
  • No valuation is made here. The range is the finding: 15.81% is what reached the three wallets and 4.53% is what is traced to public fee collectors. None of this feeds the rating rule.

-- Which intents.near accounts deliver native NEAR to NEAR's revenue wallets (dip4 native_withdraw
-- events), 2026-10-06..11-04, per day, source account and wallet. Recurring sources are fee
-- collectors; single-use sources are fresh accounts that receive NEAR unshielded from the Confidential Intents
-- treasury (PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID 51e8f94d...7cbe, docs.near-intents.org) and withdraw it.
SELECT o.block_date AS day,
       JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS src,
       JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS wallet,
       COUNT(*) AS n,
       CAST(SUM(CAST(JSON_VALUE(d, '$.amount') AS BIGNUMERIC)) / 1e24 AS STRING) AS near
FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l,
  UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(l.element, 12), '$.data')) d
WHERE o.block_date BETWEEN '2026-10-06' AND '2026-11-04'
  AND o.executor_account_id = 'intents.near'
  AND l.element LIKE 'EVENT_JSON:%native_withdraw%'
  AND JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') IN ('buybacks.multisignature.near', 'fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near',
                                         '1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near')
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3
ORDER BY 1, 2, 3

-- Exploratory (written 2026-11-12, after the freeze). Who funded the fees that reached NEAR's two fee collectors
-- (collector A 5880ad2b..., collector B 143f2bb4..., see sql/collector_inflows.sql), 2026-10-06..11-04.
-- Every dip4 transfer into A or B is classified by its sender (account_id):
--   escrow_direct  sender is the Confidential Intents treasury (PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID 51e8f94d...7cbe)
--   escrow_funded  sender received a dip4 transfer from that treasury between 2026-09-22 and the transfer's block
--   public_swap    otherwise, and the same receipt emits a dip4 token_diff for the sender (a public swap intent)
--   other          none of the above
-- Per day, collector, class and token: summed raw amounts, transfer count, distinct senders. Summed over class,
-- the rows equal data/raw/bq/collector_inflows.json.
WITH ev AS (
  SELECT o.block_date AS day, o.block_height AS h, o.receipt_id AS rid,
         l.element AS el
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l
  WHERE o.block_date BETWEEN '2026-09-22' AND '2026-11-04'
    AND o.executor_account_id = 'intents.near'
    AND l.element LIKE 'EVENT_JSON:{"standard":"dip4"%'
    AND (l.element LIKE '%"event":"transfer"%' OR l.element LIKE '%"event":"token_diff"%')
),
rows_ AS (
  SELECT day, h, rid,
         REGEXP_EXTRACT(el, r'"event":"([a-z_]+)"') AS event,
         JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS sender, JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS receiver,
         JSON_QUERY(d, '$.tokens') AS tokens
  FROM ev, UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(el, 12), '$.data')) d
),
funded AS (  -- first escrow funding of each account
  SELECT receiver AS acct, MIN(h) AS first_h
  FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'transfer' AND sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver
),
diffs AS (SELECT DISTINCT rid, sender FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'token_diff'),
inflow AS (
  SELECT r.day, r.receiver AS collector, r.sender, r.tokens,
         CASE WHEN r.sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' THEN 'escrow_direct'
              WHEN f.first_h IS NOT NULL AND f.first_h <= r.h THEN 'escrow_funded'
              WHEN dd.rid IS NOT NULL THEN 'public_swap'
              ELSE 'other' END AS class
  FROM rows_ r
  LEFT JOIN funded f ON f.acct = r.sender
  LEFT JOIN diffs dd ON dd.rid = r.rid AND dd.sender = r.sender
  WHERE r.event = 'transfer' AND r.day >= '2026-10-06'
    AND r.receiver IN ('5880ad2b362620fadf759cbceb1cd5737ce8c6ed7fb8e9942881e6731f9247dd',
                       '143f2bb439575c286f4f585163f0e5fb4e464f8ce57ec06ae80741407d3cfb31')
)
SELECT day, collector, class, REGEXP_EXTRACT(kv, r'^"([^"]+)"') AS token,
       CAST(SUM(CAST(REGEXP_EXTRACT(kv, r':"([0-9]+)"$') AS BIGNUMERIC)) AS STRING) AS amount,
       COUNT(*) AS n, COUNT(DISTINCT sender) AS senders
FROM inflow, UNNEST(REGEXP_EXTRACT_ALL(tokens, r'"[^"]+":"[0-9]+"')) AS kv
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3, 4
ORDER BY 1, 2, 3, 4

-- Exploratory (written 2026-11-12, after the freeze). Who funded the fees that reached NEAR's two fee collectors
-- (collector A 5880ad2b..., collector B 143f2bb4..., see sql/collector_inflows.sql), 2026-10-06..11-04.

--   other          none of the above

--   n_sender_single_tx    transfers whose sender sent exactly one transfer to A or B in the window
--   n_sender_single_day   ... whose sender sent to A or B on one day only
--   n_funded_once         ... whose sender received exactly one transfer from the treasury since 2026-09-22
--   n_no_other_inflow     ... whose sender received no dip4 transfer from any other account since 2026-09-22
--   lag_blocks_q          quartiles of blocks from the sender's first treasury funding to the transfer

         l.element AS el
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l
  WHERE o.block_date BETWEEN '2026-09-22' AND '2026-11-04'

    AND (l.element LIKE '%"event":"transfer"%' OR l.element LIKE '%"event":"token_diff"%')
),
rows_ AS (
  SELECT day, h, rid,
         REGEXP_EXTRACT(el, r'"event":"([a-z_]+)"') AS event,
         JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS sender, JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS receiver,
         JSON_QUERY(d, '$.tokens') AS tokens
  FROM ev, UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(el, 12), '$.data')) d
),
funded AS (  -- first escrow funding of each account
  SELECT receiver AS acct, MIN(h) AS first_h
  FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'transfer' AND sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver
),
diffs AS (SELECT DISTINCT rid, sender FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'token_diff'),
inflow AS (
  SELECT r.day, r.h, r.receiver AS collector, r.sender, r.tokens, f.first_h, dd.rid IS NOT NULL AS has_diff,
         CASE WHEN r.sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' THEN 'escrow_direct'
              WHEN f.first_h IS NOT NULL AND f.first_h <= r.h THEN 'escrow_funded'
              WHEN dd.rid IS NOT NULL THEN 'public_swap'
              ELSE 'other' END AS class
  FROM rows_ r
  LEFT JOIN funded f ON f.acct = r.sender
  LEFT JOIN diffs dd ON dd.rid = r.rid AND dd.sender = r.sender
  WHERE r.event = 'transfer' AND r.day >= '2026-10-06'
    AND r.receiver IN ('5880ad2b362620fadf759cbceb1cd5737ce8c6ed7fb8e9942881e6731f9247dd',
                       '143f2bb439575c286f4f585163f0e5fb4e464f8ce57ec06ae80741407d3cfb31')
)

, s AS (SELECT sender, COUNT(*) AS n_tx, COUNT(DISTINCT day) AS n_days FROM inflow GROUP BY sender),
fo AS (SELECT receiver AS acct, COUNT(*) AS n_fund FROM rows_ WHERE event='transfer'
       AND sender='51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver),
oth AS (SELECT receiver AS acct, COUNT(*) AS n_in_other FROM rows_ WHERE event='transfer'

SELECT i.day >= '2026-10-27' AS post, SUBSTR(i.collector,1,1) AS coll, i.class,

  COUNTIF(fo.n_fund = 1) AS n_funded_once, COUNTIF(oth.n_in_other IS NULL) AS n_no_other_inflow,

FROM inflow i JOIN s USING (sender) LEFT JOIN fo ON fo.acct=i.sender LEFT JOIN oth ON oth.acct=i.sender

Notes and sources

With every input at a value from the data and the price held flat, captured revenue on 2031-11-04 would pay for 3.5% (bear), 44.6% (base) or 277.5% (bull) of the dollar value of NEAR’s dilution. -- Daily NEAR flows into and out of staking pools, from account_changes (balances, not call args).
-- A pool's total = staked + nonstaked balance. Each change is attributed by update_reason:
--   validator_accounts_update  -> epoch staking rewards credited to the pool
--   action_receipt_gas_reward  -> 30% gas rebate to the pool contract (removed at protocol v87)
--   receipt_processing         -> deposits in minus withdrawals out (delegator flows) and the pool's own gas
-- Pools: *.poolv1.near and *.pool.near. Liquid-staking contracts (meta-pool.near, linear-protocol.near)
-- are excluded because they stake through these pools; including them would double count.
-- Window: 2026-08-07 .. 2026-11-04 (90 days, PREREG H1a); 2026-08-06 seeds the first delta.
WITH ch AS (
  SELECT block_date, affected_account_id AS acct, block_height, index_in_block, update_reason,
         (affected_account_staked_balance + affected_account_nonstaked_balance) / 1e24 AS total
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.account_changes`
  WHERE block_date BETWEEN '2026-08-06' AND '2026-11-04'
    AND (affected_account_id LIKE '%.poolv1.near' OR affected_account_id LIKE '%.pool.near')
),
d AS (
  SELECT *, total - LAG(total) OVER (PARTITION BY acct ORDER BY block_height, index_in_block) AS delta
  FROM ch
)
SELECT block_date,
       ROUND(SUM(IF(update_reason = 'validator_accounts_update', delta, 0)), 3) AS rewards_near,
       ROUND(SUM(IF(update_reason = 'action_receipt_gas_reward', delta, 0)), 3) AS gas_rebate_near,
       ROUND(SUM(IF(update_reason = 'receipt_processing' AND delta > 0, delta, 0)), 3) AS inflow_near,
       ROUND(SUM(IF(update_reason = 'receipt_processing' AND delta < 0, -delta, 0)), 3) AS outflow_near,
       COUNT(DISTINCT acct) AS pools
FROM d
WHERE block_date >= '2026-08-07' AND delta IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY block_date
ORDER BY block_date

-- Sanity check for staking_flows_daily: total NEAR held by staking pools at the end of
-- 2026-08-06 and of 2026-11-04 (last balance change of each pool on that day).
-- Expected: end - start = rewards + gas rebate + inflow - outflow from staking_flows_daily.
WITH last_state AS (
  SELECT block_date, affected_account_id AS acct,
         (affected_account_staked_balance + affected_account_nonstaked_balance) / 1e24 AS total,
         affected_account_staked_balance / 1e24 AS staked,
         ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY block_date, affected_account_id
                            ORDER BY block_height DESC, index_in_block DESC) AS rn
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.account_changes`
  WHERE block_date IN ('2026-08-06', '2026-11-04')
    AND (affected_account_id LIKE '%.poolv1.near' OR affected_account_id LIKE '%.pool.near')
)
SELECT block_date, COUNT(*) AS pools, ROUND(SUM(total), 0) AS pool_total_near, ROUND(SUM(staked), 0) AS pool_staked_near
FROM last_state WHERE rn = 1
GROUP BY block_date ORDER BY block_date

Notes and sources

Net exchange inflow lies between 4.42M and 152.45M NEAR, on either side of the 8.0098M NEAR minted, so the exchange labels cannot settle H1b.

What it shows: Net exchange inflow of NEAR (native plus wNEAR) into labelled exchange accounts over the 90 days 2026-08-07 to 2026-11-04, as a range: 4.42M NEAR if unlabelled receivers are not exchange-bound, 152.45M NEAR if every unlabelled top-100 receiver is.

What it does not show: That any of this NEAR was sold, or by whom (NEAR is fungible): it counts only labelled centralized-exchange accounts, so 1Click, a non-custodial route out of NEAR, is reported beside H1b and never inside it.

  • Only 20.0% of the top-100 native-NEAR receivers carry an exchange label (10.0% a cited one; the rest are labelled by our sweep rule), below the 50% the pre-registration requires, so H1b is confirmed only if it is true at both bounds. The lower bound is under gross issuance and the upper bound is far over it, so the labels cannot settle it.
  • The upper bound is mechanical: it treats every unlabelled top-100 receiver as an exchange, including accounts that are probably not.
  • The fourth bar of Panel A (190.71M) is longer than the upper bound (152.45M) although it leaves two accounts out: it is a side measure and does not tighten the bound. It drops the transfers between the sourced non-exchange accounts among the unlabelled top-100 receivers (here intents.near and a DAO account) and accounts outside the set, which is how pipeline/h1b.py approximates taking them out of the set counted as exchange-bound. More NEAR left the set through those transfers than entered it, so dropping them raises the total. The test uses only the lower and upper bounds.
  • Labels are snapshots dated 2026-11-06, so exchange addresses retired before then may be missing, which biases the lower bound down. No citable NEAR address was found for OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, HTX, Crypto.com, Upbit or Bithumb.

    • Avoid signal needs G_obs < G_req ÷ 2 and H1b refuted. Now: G_obs < G_req ÷ 2 is no and H1b refuted is no (it is unsettled), so the avoid signal is not met.
    • No signal otherwise, which is the case. The rule gives no signal.
  • G_req is the annual growth in captured Intents revenue (4.84M USD over the last 90 days, DefiLlama) needed for it to equal the USD value of net new supply by 2031-11-04. G_obs is the growth of the last 180 days over the 180 before, annualized.

-- events), 2026-10-06..11-04, per day, source account and wallet. Recurring sources are fee

       JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS src,
       JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS wallet,
       COUNT(*) AS n,

FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l,
  UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(l.element, 12), '$.data')) d

About the data

A pre-registered token flow statement for NEAR that anyone can re-run. make site runs python3 -m pipeline.compute and python3 -m pipeline.render in one command, rebuilding every number and chart from the raw snapshots in data/raw/, offline. Under each chart you’ll find the query behind it and the exact numbers it plots; to check one, open its Data table and follow Reproduce.

  • Net exchange inflow as one number (H1b, H1c). Only 20.0% of the top-100 receivers carry an exchange label (10.0% a cited one; the rest are labelled by our sweep rule). The binding gap is labelling: the flows are in the data, but no citable NEAR address was found for OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, HTX, Crypto.com, Upbit or Bithumb, and nothing is labelled from its name alone.
  • Which fee stream pays the escrow releases. 11.25 points of the ladder arrive as unshields from the Confidential Intents escrow. The public chain does not show which fee stream funds them; a per-stream attestation by NEAR would turn the range into one number.
  • NEAR’s share of partner fees. On partner swaps with no buyback entry the ledger puts NEAR’s share, as it reaches the buyback wallet, at no more than 22.4% (19.6% on the tighter reading) but does not measure it.
  • The other half of Intents. 1Click is 55.2% of DefiLlama’s volume on the halved basis (49.7% to 52.9% on one-sided volume, taking confidential solver shields as one-sided confidential volume; exploratory, docs/confidential-flow.md). near.com, direct solver flow and other integrations have a fee structure the Explorer API does not show, and we do not extrapolate.
  • Why the usage step happened. BigQuery shows that Sweat Economy’s signers account for 98.7% of the fall in daily transactions on 2026-04-02, and that HOT Wallet’s .tg accounts account for 98.5% of the fall in active accounts over a year (exploratory, docs/usage.md). Why either happened is not in the data, and no sybil or bot filter is applied.
  • Who the sellers were. NEAR is fungible, so every flow here is a market-wide balance. Nothing says which coins were sold.
  • What NEAR is worth as an L1. The investment case sets NEAR’s whole market cap against Intents’ captured revenue. What the L1 itself is worth (its fees, which burn, staking, any monetary premium) is not computed, so the growth the price assumes is what Intents would need if it carried the price alone.
  • Longer-dated predictions P1 to P3. They resolve on 2027-05-04 and 2027-11-04 and cannot be scored yet.
  • HSP-029’s issuance cut. Its on-chain timelock ends on 2026-11-16, after the submission deadline; we added that date up from the proposal’s voting start, voting period and timelock period, and two published sources give the same date (PREREG-ADDENDUM.md, 2026-11-12 23:50 UTC entry, item 3). A House of Stake Directors’ decision on execution follows the timelock, and the proposal text also says the cut needs validator adoption through a protocol upgrade. G_req assumes the ramp; we state it as an open question and do not predict it.
  • Earlier work. An earlier analysis of 14 accounts that reach one unlabelled hub (THESIS.md, the supply-side section, and docs/supply-side.md) is exploratory and is not reproduced here; the repository does not record when it was done or whether it preceded the freeze (PREREG-ADDENDUM.md, 2026-11-08 02:03 UTC entry, item 5). Limits

These come from the Limits sections of THESIS.md and docs/*.md and from PREREG-ADDENDUM.md. Escrow funding hops are traced per single-use delivery from 2026-10-06, which a test checks against data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv.

Show the limits
  • Collector roles. Collector A’s role (the explicit buyback fee) is cross-checked: its priced inflows are 96.5% of the fees the Explorer lists. Collector B’s role (a share of quote improvement) is inferred from committed, re-checkable per-swap evidence: it is paid a variable cut on 43 of 195 sampled swaps across channels. The quote it would be measured against is not in the Explorer API, so the role is consistent with the docs and unproven.
  • Partner numbers rest on one key-gated source, the Explorer API.
  • Sampling. Fee mechanics come from 195 swaps sampled from hours inside the window (3 of the four sampled hours returned swaps) across 55 channels, a small sample. The 30-day totals reconcile.
  • Pro-rata assumption. Collector B’s quote improvement is allocated to SwapKit by volume share. SwapKit swaps do pay collector B (9 of the 67 sampled), so the allocation has support, but its size is not measured.
  • Unpriced tokens are left out of the collector values: 87.8% of collector A’s transfers and 91.0% of collector B’s are priced, so both figures are lower bounds.
  • Escrow funding before 2026-10-06 is mostly sample-attributed.
  • Exchange labels are snapshots dated 2026-11-06. Exchange addresses retired before then may be missing, which biases the lower bound down.
  • The name-filter check behind the lst-pool.near sensitivity read a sample of validator sets, from some of the epochs.
  • Investment-case assumptions. All of it is exploratory and dated in the addendum.
