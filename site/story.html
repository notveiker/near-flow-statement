Notes and sources

NEAR delivered to NEAR’s three Intents revenue wallets equalled 15.81% of net issuance; only 4.53 points trace to public fee collectors and 11.25 arrive as unshields from the Confidential Intents escrow, fee stream unattested.

What it shows: How much of NEAR’s net issuance (88,295 NEAR a day over the 30 days 2026-10-06 to 2026-11-04) was matched by NEAR delivered to the three revenue wallets, split by how it arrived: 4.53% from two recurring public fee collectors and 11.25% released from the Confidential Intents escrow, plus 0.03% from one other source.

What it does not show: Which fee stream pays for the escrow releases: the NEAR verifiably arrived, but the public chain cannot split the 11.25 points between the two candidate NEAR-side streams that fit, so, if those three are all of NEAR’s Intents revenue wallets, measured value capture is a range, 4.53% to 15.81% of issuance.

  • The two candidate readings are near.com’s fees and the Confidential Infrastructure Fee. A per-stream attestation by NEAR would turn the range into one number. Nothing here alleges misconduct. This is consistent with a privacy feature working as documented, and the question is auditability.
  • The rungs are not nested: collectors, escrow and one other source add up to all three wallets, and the buyback-wallet rung is a subset of the total.
  • Not all of the collector rung is public settlement. Of its 4.53 points, 0.56 were fees paid by single-use accounts the escrow had funded (exploratory, every transfer into the collectors classified by sender), so 11.81 points passed through the escrow in all, and counting only publicly settled swaps the floor is 3.97%. By the last day, 96.0% of collector A’s fee value came from escrow-funded accounts.
  • The total matches DefiLlama: 418.8K NEAR is 100.9% of its reported Intents revenue for the same days. DefiLlama’s adapter takes the same three wallets from NEAR’s own Dune query, so it is the same count: it checks our pricing and wallet list and gives no independent check of the wallets. Even the top of the range covers about a sixth of issuance, and the buyback wallet holds its NEAR: none of it has been burned.
  • No valuation is made here. The range is the finding: 15.81% is what reached the three wallets and 4.53% is what is traced to public fee collectors. None of this feeds the rating rule.

sql/buyback_withdraw_sources.sql 1,162 bytes

-- Which intents.near accounts deliver native NEAR to NEAR's revenue wallets (dip4 native_withdraw
-- events), 2026-10-06..11-04, per day, source account and wallet. Recurring sources are fee
-- collectors; single-use sources are fresh accounts that receive NEAR unshielded from the Confidential Intents
-- treasury (PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID 51e8f94d...7cbe, docs.near-intents.org) and withdraw it.
SELECT o.block_date AS day,
       JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS src,
       JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS wallet,
       COUNT(*) AS n,
       CAST(SUM(CAST(JSON_VALUE(d, '$.amount') AS BIGNUMERIC)) / 1e24 AS STRING) AS near
FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l,
  UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(l.element, 12), '$.data')) d
WHERE o.block_date BETWEEN '2026-10-06' AND '2026-11-04'
  AND o.executor_account_id = 'intents.near'
  AND l.element LIKE 'EVENT_JSON:%native_withdraw%'
  AND JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') IN ('buybacks.multisignature.near', 'fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near',
                                         '1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near')
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3
ORDER BY 1, 2, 3

sql/collector_inflow_funders.sql 2,990 bytes

-- Exploratory (written 2026-11-12, after the freeze). Who funded the fees that reached NEAR's two fee collectors
-- (collector A 5880ad2b..., collector B 143f2bb4..., see sql/collector_inflows.sql), 2026-10-06..11-04.
-- Every dip4 transfer into A or B is classified by its sender (account_id):
--   escrow_direct  sender is the Confidential Intents treasury (PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID 51e8f94d...7cbe)
--   escrow_funded  sender received a dip4 transfer from that treasury between 2026-09-22 and the transfer's block
--   public_swap    otherwise, and the same receipt emits a dip4 token_diff for the sender (a public swap intent)
--   other          none of the above
-- Per day, collector, class and token: summed raw amounts, transfer count, distinct senders. Summed over class,
-- the rows equal data/raw/bq/collector_inflows.json.
WITH ev AS (
  SELECT o.block_date AS day, o.block_height AS h, o.receipt_id AS rid,
         l.element AS el
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l
  WHERE o.block_date BETWEEN '2026-09-22' AND '2026-11-04'
    AND o.executor_account_id = 'intents.near'
    AND l.element LIKE 'EVENT_JSON:{"standard":"dip4"%'
    AND (l.element LIKE '%"event":"transfer"%' OR l.element LIKE '%"event":"token_diff"%')
),
rows_ AS (
  SELECT day, h, rid,
         REGEXP_EXTRACT(el, r'"event":"([a-z_]+)"') AS event,
         JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS sender, JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS receiver,
         JSON_QUERY(d, '$.tokens') AS tokens
  FROM ev, UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(el, 12), '$.data')) d
),
funded AS (  -- first escrow funding of each account
  SELECT receiver AS acct, MIN(h) AS first_h
  FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'transfer' AND sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver
),
diffs AS (SELECT DISTINCT rid, sender FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'token_diff'),
inflow AS (
  SELECT r.day, r.receiver AS collector, r.sender, r.tokens,
         CASE WHEN r.sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' THEN 'escrow_direct'
              WHEN f.first_h IS NOT NULL AND f.first_h <= r.h THEN 'escrow_funded'
              WHEN dd.rid IS NOT NULL THEN 'public_swap'
              ELSE 'other' END AS class
  FROM rows_ r
  LEFT JOIN funded f ON f.acct = r.sender
  LEFT JOIN diffs dd ON dd.rid = r.rid AND dd.sender = r.sender
  WHERE r.event = 'transfer' AND r.day >= '2026-10-06'
    AND r.receiver IN ('5880ad2b362620fadf759cbceb1cd5737ce8c6ed7fb8e9942881e6731f9247dd',
                       '143f2bb439575c286f4f585163f0e5fb4e464f8ce57ec06ae80741407d3cfb31')
)
SELECT day, collector, class, REGEXP_EXTRACT(kv, r'^"([^"]+)"') AS token,
       CAST(SUM(CAST(REGEXP_EXTRACT(kv, r':"([0-9]+)"$') AS BIGNUMERIC)) AS STRING) AS amount,
       COUNT(*) AS n, COUNT(DISTINCT sender) AS senders
FROM inflow, UNNEST(REGEXP_EXTRACT_ALL(tokens, r'"[^"]+":"[0-9]+"')) AS kv
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3, 4
ORDER BY 1, 2, 3, 4

sql/collector_funder_checks.sql 4,260 bytes

-- Exploratory (written 2026-11-12, after the freeze). Who funded the fees that reached NEAR's two fee collectors
-- (collector A 5880ad2b..., collector B 143f2bb4..., see sql/collector_inflows.sql), 2026-10-06..11-04.
-- Every dip4 transfer into A or B is classified by its sender (account_id):
--   escrow_direct  sender is the Confidential Intents treasury (PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID 51e8f94d...7cbe)
--   escrow_funded  sender received a dip4 transfer from that treasury between 2026-09-22 and the transfer's block
--   public_swap    otherwise, and the same receipt emits a dip4 token_diff for the sender (a public swap intent)
--   other          none of the above
-- Checks of that classification, per period (post = from 2026-10-27), collector (first character) and class:
--   n_with_diff           transfers whose receipt carries a token_diff for the sender
--   n_sender_single_tx    transfers whose sender sent exactly one transfer to A or B in the window
--   n_sender_single_day   ... whose sender sent to A or B on one day only
--   n_funded_once         ... whose sender received exactly one transfer from the treasury since 2026-09-22
--   n_no_other_inflow     ... whose sender received no dip4 transfer from any other account since 2026-09-22
--   lag_blocks_q          quartiles of blocks from the sender's first treasury funding to the transfer
WITH ev AS (
  SELECT o.block_date AS day, o.block_height AS h, o.receipt_id AS rid,
         l.element AS el
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us.execution_outcomes` o, UNNEST(o.logs.list) l
  WHERE o.block_date BETWEEN '2026-09-22' AND '2026-11-04'
    AND o.executor_account_id = 'intents.near'
    AND l.element LIKE 'EVENT_JSON:{"standard":"dip4"%'
    AND (l.element LIKE '%"event":"transfer"%' OR l.element LIKE '%"event":"token_diff"%')
),
rows_ AS (
  SELECT day, h, rid,
         REGEXP_EXTRACT(el, r'"event":"([a-z_]+)"') AS event,
         JSON_VALUE(d, '$.account_id') AS sender, JSON_VALUE(d, '$.receiver_id') AS receiver,
         JSON_QUERY(d, '$.tokens') AS tokens
  FROM ev, UNNEST(JSON_QUERY_ARRAY(SUBSTR(el, 12), '$.data')) d
),
funded AS (  -- first escrow funding of each account
  SELECT receiver AS acct, MIN(h) AS first_h
  FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'transfer' AND sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver
),
diffs AS (SELECT DISTINCT rid, sender FROM rows_ WHERE event = 'token_diff'),
inflow AS (
  SELECT r.day, r.h, r.receiver AS collector, r.sender, r.tokens, f.first_h, dd.rid IS NOT NULL AS has_diff,
         CASE WHEN r.sender = '51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' THEN 'escrow_direct'
              WHEN f.first_h IS NOT NULL AND f.first_h <= r.h THEN 'escrow_funded'
              WHEN dd.rid IS NOT NULL THEN 'public_swap'
              ELSE 'other' END AS class
  FROM rows_ r
  LEFT JOIN funded f ON f.acct = r.sender
  LEFT JOIN diffs dd ON dd.rid = r.rid AND dd.sender = r.sender
  WHERE r.event = 'transfer' AND r.day >= '2026-10-06'
    AND r.receiver IN ('5880ad2b362620fadf759cbceb1cd5737ce8c6ed7fb8e9942881e6731f9247dd',
                       '143f2bb439575c286f4f585163f0e5fb4e464f8ce57ec06ae80741407d3cfb31')
)

, s AS (SELECT sender, COUNT(*) AS n_tx, COUNT(DISTINCT day) AS n_days FROM inflow GROUP BY sender),
fo AS (SELECT receiver AS acct, COUNT(*) AS n_fund FROM rows_ WHERE event='transfer'
       AND sender='51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver),
oth AS (SELECT receiver AS acct, COUNT(*) AS n_in_other FROM rows_ WHERE event='transfer'
       AND sender!='51e8f94d77b5e90dc9852ca6113771e11e8382ce69473a75e313e41665de7cbe' GROUP BY receiver)
SELECT i.day >= '2026-10-27' AS post, SUBSTR(i.collector,1,1) AS coll, i.class,
  COUNT(*) AS n, COUNTIF(i.has_diff) AS n_with_diff, COUNT(DISTINCT i.sender) AS senders,
  COUNTIF(s.n_tx = 1) AS n_sender_single_tx, COUNTIF(s.n_days = 1) AS n_sender_single_day,
  COUNTIF(fo.n_fund = 1) AS n_funded_once, COUNTIF(oth.n_in_other IS NULL) AS n_no_other_inflow,
  APPROX_QUANTILES(i.h - i.first_h, 4) AS lag_blocks_q
FROM inflow i JOIN s USING (sender) LEFT JOIN fo ON fo.acct=i.sender LEFT JOIN oth ON oth.acct=i.sender
GROUP BY 1,2,3 ORDER BY 1,2,3

Notes and sources

For NEAR’s price to rise 15% a year to 2031-11-04 and its market cap then to be 25 times its captured revenue, captured revenue must grow from $19.6M a year to $424.0M, 84.9% a year (54.0% to 131.7% across the grid), if Intents carried the price alone. The cross-chain venues tracked here grew 13.4% last year, all of it Intents’ own growth (×9.0): without Intents the venues’ volume changed by -53.4%. Over four years the set grew 47.1% a year, 15.4% without Intents.

What it shows: A reverse DCF: the growth in captured Intents revenue that today’s market cap implies, for a required return and a revenue multiple in 2031 (both assumptions, shown as a grid), with supply growth on the rating rule’s own path, against what the cross-chain swap market has done.

What it does not show: A valuation of NEAR as a whole. NEAR’s market cap also prices the L1 (its fees, which burn, staking and any monetary premium); here all of it is set against Intents’ captured revenue, so the growth shown is what Intents would need if it carried the price alone.

  • For comparison, THORChain’s market cap is 9.8× its gross fees and Chainflip’s 1.8× (see the peer card). The grid’s multiples are on captured revenue; the peers’ multiples on what reaches their token holders would be higher and are not computed here.
  • Last year’s growth of the set is all Intents’ growth, and part of it is DefiLlama’s change on 2026-09-01 in what it counts as Intents volume (docs/leg-count.md): as a measure of the market it is generous to NEAR.
  • The market’s growth depends on the window: 76.9% a year over three years starts at the low of $9.6B; the annual volumes were $11.3B, $9.6B, $55.1B, $46.7B and $53.0B (oldest first). The two-year rate is negative and is drawn at zero.
  • G_obs, the rule’s observed growth in captured revenue (619.0% a year), is left off the axis: it comes from a step in the share of fees captured, while gross fees and volume grew far less.

Notes and sources

Captured revenue = cross-chain swap volume × Intents’ share × NEAR’s take. To multiply by 21.6 by 2031, with the market growing at its last-year pace (×1.87) and Intents’ share at its twelve-month high (×1.27), NEAR’s take would have to rise ×9.1, from 4.13 bp to 37.4 bp of volume: 2.5 times the whole 15.2 bp gross fee Intents charges today. Even if NEAR kept the whole gross fee, the market would have to grow 35.7% a year.

What it shows: The required multiple from the reverse DCF (base assumptions), split into its three factors: the growth of the cross-chain swap market, Intents’ share of it, and the share of volume NEAR captures as revenue, each at a value from the data, and what is left for the take rate.

What it does not show: That the take rate cannot rise: fees are a policy choice. It shows how far the current fee structure is from what the price implies, if the market grows as it has.

  • Intents’ share of the peer set over the 90 days was 61.7%; its twelve-month high was 78.6% (2026-06) and its low 48.5% (2026-04).
  • Each factor is on DefiLlama’s reported volume, which counts both legs of some swaps and, from 2026-09-01, more of Intents’ volume (docs/leg-count.md). Only the full product, the revenue, is unaffected. Last year’s market growth is all Intents’ and partly that change, so it is generous to NEAR, and the take needed is, if anything, understated.
  • Take and gross fee are DefiLlama revenue and fees ÷ volume, on the reported basis.

4. Three futures from the data

Figure 4Exploratory#

Dilution paid for in 2031: 3.5% bear, 44.6% base, 277.5% bull

Notes and sources

With every input at a value from the data and the price held flat, captured revenue on 2031-11-04 would pay for 3.5% (bear), 44.6% (base) or 277.5% (bull) of the dollar value of NEAR’s dilution. The bull case’s $228.6M a year is 53.9% of the $424.0M the price would assume at 15% and 25×; at the three-year market pace it would be 136.0%. Market growth and NEAR’s price move coverage most: from 21.6% to 163.8% and from 27.8% to 169.8%.

What it shows: Three futures for captured revenue (market growth × Intents’ share × NEAR’s take), each input at a value from the data, run against the rating rule’s supply path at its constant (flat) price; then the base case with one input at a time at its low and high value. Coverage is what captured revenue would pay for: not all of it reaches the buyback wallet, and none of it is burned.

What it does not show: Probabilities: the scenarios are not weighted (the Monte Carlo card gives a distribution). Nor a new fee stream, a partner change or a regime the data have not seen.

  • Bear: market at its two-year pace, share at its twelve-month low (2026-04), take at its lowest month since the fee switch (2026-04), no HSP-029. Base: last-year market growth and the last 90 days’ share and take, with HSP-029. Bull: four-year market pace, twelve-month-high share (2026-06), highest month’s take (2026-10), with HSP-029.
  • Coverage moves in inverse proportion to NEAR’s price, because each dollar of revenue buys back less NEAR at a higher price: the 365-day range, $0.96 to $5.89, moves the base case from 27.8% to 169.8%.
  • Which window anchors each case was chosen after the annual volumes, monthly shares and monthly take rates were computed (PREREG-ADDENDUM.md, 2026-11-14 entries); the card on what the price assumes lists every window, so a reader can substitute another.
  • The bull case grows the whole peer set at its four-year pace and also lifts Intents’ share, so it counts some of Intents’ share gains twice. Its revenue growth, 63.4% a year, falls short of what the price would assume at 25× and exceeds it only at 50× (54.0% at 10% a year, 61.0% at 15%).
  • At today’s market cap, NEAR would trade at 131.1× its 2031 captured revenue in the base case and 21.1× in the bull case.

What it shows: The frozen rating rule’s question (does captured revenue reach the USD value of net new supply by 2031-11-04?) with a distribution in place of one price and one growth rate: 10,000 paths, each month drawing one of the 11 monthly changes in Intents volume and NEAR’s mean price (2025-12 to 2026-10) together, against the rule’s own supply path.

What it does not show: A forecast of NEAR’s price or a valuation. Eleven months of data cannot pin a five-year trend: the bands are as wide as the data make them, the take rate is held where it is, and no new fee stream, partner change or regime is modelled.

  • The volume drift comes from one month: the 2026-08 to 2026-09 change spans DefiLlama’s 2026-09-01 change in what it counts as Intents volume. Without that month volume changed by -4.5% a month, the 2031 median is 1.5% and 3.3% of paths cover all dilution. The forecasts M1 to M4 were dated with the month in and are scored as dated; the thesis does not rest on them (PREREG-ADDENDUM.md, 2026-11-14 entries).
  • Volume and price move together in the data: the correlation of their monthly log changes is 0.54, so each month’s two changes are drawn together.
  • The price drift is removed because NEAR’s mean price rose 7.4% a month on average over those months; replaying that for five years would assume the trend continues. With it, the 2031 median is 0.6%: a higher price means each dollar of revenue buys back less NEAR.
  • Volume keeps its sample drift, 0.8% a month. The take rate is captured revenue ÷ volume over the last 90 days, 4.13 bp, held fixed; coverage moves in proportion to it.
  • P3 (frozen in PREREG.md with probability 35.0%) asks whether coverage is at least 25% on 2027-11-04. The model, built after the freeze, gives 39.6%; P3 is scored on the data alone.
  • Coverage today here is computed in USD at one price, as the rating rule does; the ladder’s 15.81%-of-issuance figure counts NEAR as it arrived, so the two differ slightly.

The view

Intents led its market over the year to 2026-11-04, but as a claim on Intents’ fees NEAR is priced for more growth than the data show at today’s fees: the bull case reaches 53.9% of the revenue the price would assume.

  • Base case: captured revenue would pay for 44.6% of NEAR’s dilution in 2031.
  • To close the gap in today’s market, NEAR would have to capture 2.5× Intents’ whole gross fee.
  • Pre-registered: net staking outflow was 6.56× staking rewards, where H1a predicted under 0.5×; exchange flows come out as ranges the labels cannot settle, so the frozen rating rule gives no signal.
  • Forward tests, scored 2026-11-14: 2 of the four frozen predictions came true and 2 did not; the Monte Carlo’s forecasts are dated in the addendum and resolve from 2027.
  • Carried to 2026-11-13 with the same code: the price assumes 79.8% a year, NEAR would need 1.6× Intents’ whole gross fee, and the base case pays for 62.2% of the dilution in 2031.
