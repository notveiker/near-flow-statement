# How much of NEAR's value capture can you verify?

*An investment thesis for NEAR, built as a token flow statement. Not investment advice. Window: the 30 days to 2026-11-04 for value capture, the 90 days 2026-08-07 → 11-04 for supply. Every number marked with an `n:` comment is recomputed from committed data by `make reproduce`.*

## Bottom line

Terms such as H1a, Hold, the ladder and the escrow are defined in the [glossary](docs/glossary.md).

1. **Pre-registered: one fail, two inconclusive; the rating is Hold, which carries no view.** H1a (most staking rewards stay staked) fails: net staking outflow was 6.56<!--n:h1a_ratio_as_written_x-->× staking rewards, where a pass needed under 0.5×. H1b (less NEAR reaches exchanges than is minted) and H1c (identified buying is at least 25% of net exchange inflow) are inconclusive: only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 NEAR receivers carry an exchange label (10.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_tier1_count_pct-->% a cited one), below the 50% the pre-registration requires. The frozen rating rule therefore outputs **Hold**, which carries no view on NEAR.
2. **Exploratory, chosen after the freeze: a range, not a number.** Over the 30 days to 2026-11-04, the NEAR that verifiably reached NEAR's three Intents revenue wallets was 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance. Only 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points of that trace on chain to public fee collectors; 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> arrived as releases from the Confidential Intents escrow, whose fee stream is unattested, and 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> came from one other recurring source. Not all of the 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points come from public settlement: tracing every transfer into the two collectors to its sender gives 3.97<!--n:ladder_collectors_public_swap_pct_of_issuance--> points from publicly settled swaps and 0.56<!--n:ladder_collectors_escrow_funded_pct_of_issuance--> from fees paid by single-use accounts that the escrow had funded moments before (section 1).
3. **Exploratory: the usable result is a range (not a valuation).** The value capture that can be verified is 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->% to 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance, if those three are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets; counting only fees from publicly settled swaps, the floor is 3.97<!--n:ladder_collectors_public_swap_pct_of_issuance-->%. A per-stream attestation by NEAR of which fees fund the escrow releases would turn the range into one number. Arrival is not removal: none of the three wallets burns, stakes or locks its NEAR. 11.0<!--n:ladder_buyback_only_pct_of_issuance--> of the points arrived in the buyback wallet; the other 4.81 arrived in the front-end fund (`fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`), which has never sent NEAR to the buyback wallet and whose signers can change its rules or its code with one vote. Forwarding the fees at all is a permission in the 1Click terms ("may be directed"), not an obligation. The ratio also moves with price and issuance policy: from the first to the last 30-day block of the supply window, captured revenue in dollars (DefiLlama) rose 118.9% while the NEAR arriving in the wallets fell 13.8%, and against the issuance rate before the 2025-10-29 inflation cut the same NEAR would be about 7.9% of net issuance ([second review](docs/second-review.md), sections 1 and 5).
4. **Evidence against (exploratory): partners, usage, valuation.** Listed partner fees were 1.44× to 2.22<!--n:partners_kept_vs_near_rev_high_x-->× NEAR's entire captured revenue (1.75<!--n:partners_kept_vs_near_rev_low_x-->× if none of the front-end fund's NEAR is partner money). NEAR's market cap is 199<!--n:mcap_to_rev_30d_annualized_x-->× its last 30 days of captured Intents revenue annualised (descriptive, not a valuation).

HSP-029's on-chain timelock ends on 2026-11-16 by our own arithmetic (no published text gives that date), after the submission deadline, and the rating's required growth assumes the cut is in force. ## The thesis

NEAR's net issuance (minted minus burned) is about 88,295<!--n:net_issuance_near_per_day_window--> NEAR a day. Over the 30 days to 2026-11-04, the NEAR that verifiably arrived in NEAR's three Intents revenue wallets was equal to **15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->%** of that.

Only **4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->** points of it can be traced on chain to public fee collectors. The other **11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance-->** points arrived as releases from the Confidential Intents escrow (and 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> points from one other recurring source): the NEAR is there, but which fee stream paid for it is not visible from outside. Of the 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> collector points, 0.56<!--n:ladder_collectors_escrow_funded_pct_of_issuance--> were fees paid from escrow-funded single-use accounts, which makes 11.81<!--n:ladder_escrow_all_pct_of_issuance--> points that passed through the escrow in all. So the value capture that can be verified is a range, 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->% to 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance, if those three are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets (DefiLlama's adapter lists the same three, taken from NEAR's own Dune query, so its revenue is the same count, not an independent check). A per-stream attestation by NEAR would collapse the range to one number.

The range is a measured result. It is not a valuation, and the frozen rule's Hold is not a recommendation. Value capture that does not pass through those wallets, or that cannot be seen on chain, is not in it. Arrival is not removal from supply: the buyback wallet held 2.187<!--n:buyback_wallet_near_m-->M NEAR at the last block in the committed data (223,230,811<!--n:rpc_block-->), and what happens to it next is not in the data. Even the top of the range is about a sixth of issuance, and who buys the rest is not shown by this data.

## 2. How we know it is the escrow, and what is actually missing (exploratory)

- **What an unshield looks like on chain.** A confidential withdrawal shows on chain as the escrow paying the exact amount to a fresh account, which then withdraws it to a revenue wallet. That is an observation: the documentation describes the release from the Treasury, not this pattern. In the window, all 909<!--n:escrow_funded_exact_30d_n--> such deliveries were funded by the escrow for the exact amount, to the yoctoNEAR (100.0<!--n:escrow_funded_exact_30d_pct-->%).
- **The escrow's identity is documented.** Account `51e8f94d…7cbe` is `PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID` in NEAR's Confidential Intents docs, "a production constant shared by all participants". The docs call it the Treasury and say it backs each confidential token one for one; "escrow" is our word for it.
- **Anyone can re-check it, and what the re-check does and does not show.** Every delivery is in `data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv`; a funding transfer is there too for every single-use delivery in the 30-day window (909<!--n:escrow_single_use_30d_n-->) and for a traced sample before it. `python3 -m pipeline.fetch_escrow verify --all` re-reads every row from FastNEAR and archival RPC, with no key, and its output of 2026-11-08 is committed (`data/raw/escrow/verify_all.json`). Of the 3,597<!--n:escrow_recheck_rows_n--> rows, 3,587<!--n:escrow_recheck_rows_ok_n--> pass every check and the other 10<!--n:escrow_recheck_failed_n--> fail on block height alone, one to three blocks off (the table holds the block that includes the receipt; the check compares the block that executes it), so the command exits with status 1 on the committed data. All 1,051<!--n:escrow_recheck_funding_hops_n--> funding transfers pass, including all 909<!--n:escrow_recheck_window_funding_ok_n--> of the window's single-use deliveries. The 100.0<!--n:escrow_funded_exact_30d_pct-->% above tallies two columns of the table (`funder` and `exact_amount_flag`) over the window's single-use deliveries. Which deliveries are single-use does not depend on those columns (it follows from the committed BigQuery output of the arrivals), and the re-read confirmed the recorded funding transfer of every one of them on the chain. What cannot be reproduced is the funding scan itself: its SQL, and the code that assigned the table's labels, are not in this repository, and before 2026-10-06 the funding hops are sampled. Details: [`docs/sources-checked.md`](docs/sources-checked.md), sections 2 and 3.
- **What is missing is attestation, not NEAR.** The NEAR verifiably arrived. Two candidate NEAR-side fee streams fit, and the public chain cannot split the 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> points between them:
  - **near.com's fees.** NEAR says near.com swaps "direct 100% of fees to a NEAR revenue wallet", without naming which. The same answer says swaps through third-party partners are "split 50/50 between NEAR and the partner"; the arithmetic in section 3 tests that. near.com's own code names three fee-related accounts, of which we discuss two (the third, `APP_FEE_DISTRIBUTOR`, is not discussed). `APP_FEE_RECIPIENT` (`176121bd…`) withdrew to the front-end fund (`fefundsadmin`) in every delivery of it in our table (2026-07-07 to 2026-10-21) and to the buyback wallet in none. `CONFIDENTIAL_FEE_RECIPIENT` (`60a0d43b…`) appears nowhere in FastNEAR's public index of the chain. Calling them the public-mode and confidential-mode fee accounts is our reading of those names.
  - **The Confidential Infrastructure Fee.** The 1Click terms (Schedule 1 §6.2, §6.4) say Intents Technology "sets, charges, and retains" it on confidential swaps, with no share to integrators. They state no rate ("if any", §6.3), and the public fee pages do not mention confidential swaps, so the terms say who would keep such a fee, not that one is charged. Schedule 1 §1.7 adds that infrastructure fees "may be directed by the 1Click Service to a designated fee recipient in connection with NEAR ecosystem infrastructure, development and growth activities", and names no recipient. Forwarding any of it to NEAR's wallets would be a choice, not a documented fact.
- **The falsifier.** A per-stream attestation by NEAR (which fees fund which escrow releases) would turn the range into one number. Nothing here alleges misconduct: this is consistent with a privacy feature working as documented, and the question is auditability. Since 2026-10-27 it is also the only route left: most 1Click settlement is now confidential, so the public rung will thin out (Limits).

## 3. Evidence against: who else gets paid, usage, valuation context (exploratory)

**Who else gets paid (1Click, 30 days).** These numbers come from the NEAR Intents Explorer API, which needs a partner key, and from on-chain wallet arithmetic. Details: [`docs/intents-fee-flows.md`](docs/intents-fee-flows.md), [`docs/partner-concentration.md`](docs/partner-concentration.md).

- **Captured take (a pre-freeze observation, from `PREREG.md`'s table).** NEAR's captured revenue was 4.98<!--n:take_captured_bp_30d--> bp of reported volume, 32.5<!--n:capture_share_30d_pct-->% of gross fees.

## Limits

- **Second review (exploratory, 2026-11-12).** A later review re-ran parts of this analysis from the committed data. It found that the escrow share rose in a step at the end of August rather than as a trend, that the rise in the explicit fee follows a documented 1Click price change on 2026-09-03, that the front-end fund holds its 4.81 points of the ladder under its own five signers and has never forwarded NEAR to the buyback wallet, and that H1b's lower bound turns on single days. It changes no pre-registered result. Details, with a recipe for each figure: [`docs/second-review.md`](docs/second-review.md).
- **The public trail is shrinking.** NEAR Intents' changelog says that 1Click swaps settle confidentially from 2026-10-27. Collector B's daily transfers fell from 4,387 on average over 10-06 to 10-28 to 109 on 11-04, and collector A's from 7,682 to 2,739. The 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points traced to public collectors describe a settlement regime that is ending, and the per-swap method of [`docs/intents-fee-flows.md`](docs/intents-fee-flows.md) will not be repeatable on confidential swaps ([second review](docs/second-review.md), section 4). The fees themselves show it: by 11-04, 96.0<!--n:collector_a_escrow_usd_share_last_day_pct-->% of collector A's fee value came from escrow-funded accounts (section 1), so a later 30-day window will show the collector rung mostly as escrow-funded rather than public.
- **Not settled here.** Who sold the NEAR that the front-end fund bought with USDC; whether NEAR counts the front-end fund, whose signers are Aurora-named accounts, as one of its revenue wallets; whether DefiLlama's Intents volume counts both legs of each swap, which moves every take rate and the peer share by about 2×; and which fees fund the escrow releases. None of these can be computed from the committed data.
- **Collector roles.** Collector A's role (the explicit buyback fee) is cross-checked: its priced inflows are 96.5<!--n:collector_a_vs_explicit_fees_pct-->% of the fees the Explorer lists. Collector B's role (a share of quote improvement) is inferred from committed, re-checkable per-swap evidence: it is paid a variable cut on 43<!--n:fee_ev_collector_b_swaps_n--> of 195<!--n:fee_ev_swaps_n--> sampled swaps across channels. The quote it would be measured against is not in the Explorer API, so the role is consistent with the docs rather than proven.
- **Partner numbers** rest on one key-gated source (the Explorer API), and the per-swap evidence is 195<!--n:fee_ev_swaps_n--> swaps from 3<!--n:fee_ev_hours_with_swaps_n--> sampled hours.
- **Escrow funding** before 2026-10-06 is mostly sample-attributed. The identity of the escrow account and both candidate fee streams rest on NEAR's documentation, which this repository does not commit. [`docs/sources-checked.md`](docs/sources-checked.md) quotes what each page said on 2026-11-08 and marks where our wording goes beyond it; pages can change.
- **Earlier work outside this repository** is not reproduced here and, apart from the 14-account reading in section 4 (labelled exploratory), is not relied on. This repository cannot show whether that analysis preceded the freeze.
- **Rating constants.** G_req rests on assumptions committed after the freeze (section 4).
- **Usage data** are Nearblocks aggregates with undocumented definitions, not filtered for sybils or relayers, and with the 2026-04-02 step. The brief's BigQuery usage queries (daily active accounts, transaction counts, contract usage) were not run.
- **H1a** is net of deposits and includes exchange-owned stake moves.
- **H1c's numerator.** PREREG defines identified buying as the buyback wallet's balance growth, 1.48<!--n:h1c_identified_buying_m-->M NEAR over the 90 days, and H1c uses all of it. The deliveries from `intents.near` that we trace explain 0.97<!--n:bb_deliveries_90d_m-->M NEAR of it; the other 0.511<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_m-->M NEAR (34.5<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_pct-->%) is not explained by the committed data, which holds no transfers from other accounts. A read of the chain on 2026-11-08 (`docs/sources-checked.md`, 2.7; it is not in the committed data, so no figure from it is marked here) traces that remainder to ten native transfers from other accounts, almost all of it from `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, another NEAR revenue wallet, through two of that DAO's transfer proposals. So about a third of the numerator is NEAR that came from another NEAR wallet rather than from `intents.near`; where that NEAR came from, we did not trace. The verdict is inconclusive whichever numerator is used, because label coverage is below 50%.
