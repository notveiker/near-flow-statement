# How much of NEAR's value capture can you verify?

*An investment thesis for NEAR, built as a token flow statement. Not investment advice. Window: the 30 days to 2026-11-04 for value capture, the 90 days 2026-08-07 → 11-04 for supply. Every number marked with an `n:` comment is recomputed from committed data by `make reproduce`.*

**Where to look.** The claim is in [the thesis in one paragraph](#the-thesis-in-one-paragraph). Evidence for it is sections 1 and 2; evidence against it is section 3 (who else gets paid, usage, valuation context) and section 4 (the supply side). What would change our mind is section 5, and the limits close the file. **Labels:** only the tests in section 4 are pre-registered. Everything else is exploratory and was chosen after the freeze ([`PREREG-ADDENDUM.md`](PREREG-ADDENDUM.md)); section 4's additions are labelled where they appear.

**The pre-registered result comes first.** H1a fails: net staking outflow was 6.56<!--n:h1a_ratio_as_written_x-->× staking rewards, where a pass needed under 0.5×. H1b, whether less NEAR reached exchanges than was minted, is **inconclusive**: only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 NEAR receivers carry an exchange label (10.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_tier1_count_pct-->% a cited one; the rest are labelled by our own sweep rule), below the 50% the pre-registration requires, so net exchange inflow is a range, 4.42<!--n:h1b_net_inflow_lower_m-->M to 152.45<!--n:h1b_net_inflow_upper_m-->M NEAR, against 8.0098<!--n:h1a_gross_issuance_90d_m-->M NEAR minted. H1c is inconclusive. Our priors that they would pass were 0.70, 0.55 and 0.40; one test failed and two could not be decided. The frozen rating rule therefore outputs **Hold**. That output carries no view on NEAR: below 50% label coverage H1b can come out pass or inconclusive but never fail, and observed revenue growth (619.0<!--n:rating_g_obs_pct-->% a year; 71.4<!--n:rating_g_obs_near_pct-->% in NEAR terms) is far above the rule's Avoid line (16.6<!--n:rating_g_avoid_line_pct-->% a year, half of the required 33.2<!--n:rating_g_req_pct-->%), so the rule could only ever have output Buy or Hold, and had H1b failed it would also have said Hold.

## The thesis in one paragraph

NEAR's net issuance (minted minus burned) is about 88,295<!--n:net_issuance_near_per_day_window--> NEAR a day. Over the 30 days to 2026-11-04, the NEAR that verifiably arrived in NEAR's three Intents revenue wallets was equal to **15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->%** of that. Only **4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->** points of it can be traced on chain to public fee collectors. The other **11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance-->** points arrived as releases from the Confidential Intents escrow (and 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> points from one other recurring source): the NEAR is there, but which fee stream paid for it is not visible from outside. So the value capture that can be verified is a range, 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->% to 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of issuance, and a per-stream attestation by NEAR would collapse it to one number. Even the top of the range is about a sixth of issuance, and who buys the rest is not shown by this data. Arrival is not removal from supply: the buyback wallet held 2.187<!--n:buyback_wallet_near_m-->M NEAR at the last block we read, and what happens to it next is not in the data. For an investor the usable result is a bound: the NEAR that verifiably arrived in those three wallets was about a sixth of issuance, so, if they are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets (DefiLlama's adapter lists these three, and our total reconciles to its revenue), a case that rests on Intents volume or headline buyback figures has to be discounted to that bound. It is not a valuation, and the frozen rule's Hold is not a recommendation.

- **The rungs are not nested.** Collectors + escrow + the other source = all three wallets. The buyback-wallet line is a subset of the total.
- **Denominator.** Net issuance is 88,295<!--n:net_issuance_near_per_day_window--> NEAR/day, from archival `total_supply` at blocks 218,718,000 and 223,087,766 (`data/raw/escrow/issuance_reads.json`), matched to the window. They agree closely.
- **The total reconciles with DefiLlama.** Our three-wallet total, 418.8<!--n:revwallets_near_k-->K NEAR, is 100.9<!--n:revwallets_vs_llama_rev_pct-->% of DefiLlama's reported Intents revenue for the same days (compared in USD). - **The escrow share is rising.** Share of the buyback wallet's NEAR that arrived via the escrow: Aug 28.6<!--n:escrow_share_buyback_2026_08_pct-->%, Sep 53.0<!--n:escrow_share_buyback_2026_09_pct-->%, Oct 57.4<!--n:escrow_share_buyback_2026_10_pct-->%. - **No valuation is made here.** The range is the finding, not either end. If a model needs one number, 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% is what reached the wallets and 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->% is what is traced to public fee collectors. Neither feeds the frozen rating rule.

## 2. How we know it is the escrow, and what is actually missing (exploratory)

- **What an unshield looks like on chain.** A confidential withdrawal shows on chain as the escrow paying the exact amount to a fresh account, which then withdraws it to a revenue wallet. That is an observation: the documentation describes the release from the Treasury, not this pattern. In the window, all 909<!--n:escrow_funded_exact_30d_n--> such deliveries were funded by the escrow for the exact amount, to the yoctoNEAR (100.0<!--n:escrow_funded_exact_30d_pct-->%).
- **The escrow's identity is documented.** Account `51e8f94d…7cbe` is `PRIVATE_TREASURY_ACCOUNT_ID` in NEAR's Confidential Intents docs, "a production constant shared by all participants". The docs call it the Treasury and say it backs each confidential token one for one; "escrow" is our word for it.
- **Anyone can re-check it, and what the re-check does and does not show.** Every delivery and its funding transfer is in `data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv`. `python3 -m pipeline.fetch_escrow verify --all` re-reads every row from FastNEAR and archival RPC, with no key, and its output of 2026-11-08 is committed (`data/raw/escrow/verify_all.json`). Of the 3,597<!--n:escrow_recheck_rows_n--> rows, 3,587<!--n:escrow_recheck_rows_ok_n--> pass every check and the other 10<!--n:escrow_recheck_failed_n--> fail on block height alone, one to three blocks off (the table holds the block that includes the receipt; the check compares the block that executes it), so the command exits with status 1 on the committed data. All 1,051<!--n:escrow_recheck_funding_hops_n--> funding transfers pass, including all 909<!--n:escrow_recheck_window_funding_ok_n--> of the window's single-use deliveries. The 100.0<!--n:escrow_funded_exact_30d_pct-->% above tallies two columns of the table (`funder` and `exact_amount_flag`) over the window's single-use deliveries. Which deliveries are single-use does not depend on those columns (it follows from the committed BigQuery output of the arrivals), and the re-read confirmed the recorded funding transfer of every one of them on the chain. What cannot be reproduced is the funding scan itself: its SQL, and the code that assigned the table's labels, are not in this repository, and before 2026-10-06 the funding hops are sampled. Details: [`docs/sources-checked.md`](docs/sources-checked.md), sections 2 and 3.
- **What is missing is attestation, not NEAR.** The NEAR verifiably arrived. Two candidate NEAR-side fee streams fit, and the public chain cannot split the 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> points between them:
  - **near.com's fees.** NEAR says near.com swaps "direct 100% of fees to a NEAR revenue wallet", without naming which. near.com's own code names two fee accounts. `APP_FEE_RECIPIENT` (`176121bd…`) withdrew to the front-end fund (`fefundsadmin`) in every delivery of it in our table (2026-07-07 to 2026-10-21) and to the buyback wallet in none. `CONFIDENTIAL_FEE_RECIPIENT` (`60a0d43b…`) appears nowhere in FastNEAR's public index of the chain. Calling them the public-mode and confidential-mode fee accounts is our reading of those names.
  - **The Confidential Infrastructure Fee.** The 1Click terms (Schedule 1 §6.2, §6.4) say Intents Technology "sets, charges, and retains" it on confidential swaps, with no share to integrators. They state no rate ("if any", §6.3), and the public fee pages do not mention confidential swaps, so the terms say who would keep such a fee, not that one is charged. Forwarding any of it to NEAR's wallets would be a choice.
- **The falsifier.** A per-stream attestation by NEAR (which fees fund which escrow releases) would turn the range into one number. Nothing here alleges misconduct: this is a privacy feature working as designed, and the question is auditability.

## 3. Evidence against: who else gets paid, usage, valuation context (exploratory)

**Who else gets paid (1Click, 30 days).** These numbers come from the NEAR Intents Explorer API, which needs a partner key, and from on-chain wallet arithmetic. Details: [`docs/intents-fee-flows.md`](docs/intents-fee-flows.md), [`docs/partner-concentration.md`](docs/partner-concentration.md).

- **Captured take (a pre-freeze observation, from `PREREG.md`'s table).** NEAR's captured revenue was 4.98<!--n:take_captured_bp_30d--> bp of reported volume, 32.5<!--n:capture_share_30d_pct-->% of gross fees.
- **Partners keep more than NEAR.** Partners kept at least 1.75<!--n:partners_kept_vs_near_rev_low_x-->× NEAR's entire captured revenue (1.81<!--n:partners_kept_vs_near_rev_low_tight_x-->× if the escrow NEAR in the buyback wallet is NEAR-side fee rather than partner settlement, which is unattested; neither counts the front-end fund's NEAR as partner money). On partner swaps with no buyback fee entry, at most 22.4<!--n:near_share_of_partner_fees_max_pct-->% of fees reaches NEAR's buyback wallet (19.6<!--n:near_share_of_partner_fees_max_tight_pct-->% on the tighter reading). In the per-swap sample, partners are paid their listed fee on chain: for SwapKit on 102<!--n:fee_ev_swapkit_fees_paid_onchain_n--> of 112<!--n:fee_ev_swapkit_fees_listed_n--> listed entries, where the other 10<!--n:fee_ev_swapkit_fees_zero_rate_n--> list a zero rate (nothing is owed on them). So no split comes out of a listed partner fee. Whatever 1Click charges on top, and whether it forwards any of it to NEAR, is not visible.
- **SwapKit is the volume concentration risk.** It routes 38.2<!--n:swapkit_vol_share_pct-->% of 1Click volume and paid NEAR $159<!--n:swapkit_buyback_fees_usd--> in explicit fees over 30 days. If it left, NEAR would lose about 2.0<!--n:swapkit_exit_traceable_pct-->% of captured revenue on the streams we can trace to it. The ceilings, which exist because escrow NEAR cannot be attributed, are two readings: 43.0<!--n:swapkit_exit_ceiling_pct-->% if all the NEAR unshielded from the escrow to the buyback wallet were SwapKit's, and 73.7<!--n:swapkit_exit_ceiling_all_escrow_pct-->% if the escrow NEAR reaching the front-end fund counted too.
- **NEAR's explicit fee rate rose from a low base, on falling volume.** NEAR's explicit fee inside 1Click went from 1.83<!--n:take_b1_bb_explicit_bp--> bp of volume (Aug 7 – Sep 5) to 4.08<!--n:take_b3_bb_explicit_bp--> bp (Oct 6 – Nov 4), mostly because more volume carries a buyback fee entry (23.6<!--n:take_b1_vol_with_bb_pct-->% → 43.0<!--n:take_b3_vol_with_bb_pct-->%). That is a rate, not an amount: 1Click volume was $1,442<!--n:take_b2_oneclick_usd_m-->M in the middle block and $1,104<!--n:take_b3_oneclick_usd_m-->M in the last. Partner fees were about ten times NEAR's explicit fee in the last block (40.3<!--n:take_b3_partner_bp--> bp against 4.08<!--n:take_b3_bb_explicit_bp--> bp). The pre-registered F3 predicts that the captured take (a different series: DefiLlama revenue over reported volume) falls back below 4.0 bp, because October's high "was a high, not a new level"; it is scored after 2026-11-14, and a hit would mean the rise in this bullet did not persist.
- **Volume is not the token.** Intents is 55.7<!--n:intents_share_peerset_30d_pct-->% of its cross-chain peer set by reported volume (38.6<!--n:intents_share_peerset_30d_net_pct-->% on a net basis, halving DefiLlama's Intents volume because its figure appears to count both legs of a swap; its adapter states no methodology, and the evidence for "appears" is in [`docs/intents-fee-flows.md`](docs/intents-fee-flows.md)), but only 3.5<!--n:explorer_near_side_swap_share_pct-->% of 1Click volume has NEAR on one side. 1Click was a net route out of NEAR: $24.0<!--n:explorer_out_of_near_usd_m-->M out against $14.6<!--n:explorer_into_near_usd_m-->M in.

**Usage (Nearblocks, not BigQuery).** The dashboard card `chart-usage` in `site/index.html` charts it.

- **Active accounts are falling; the transaction count is not a usable measure.** Daily active accounts averaged 61.6<!--n:active_accounts_30d_k-->K over the 30 days to 2026-11-04: -77.1<!--n:active_accounts_30d_yoy_pct-->% on the same 30 days a year earlier and -52.1<!--n:active_accounts_since_step_pct-->% on the first 30 days after 2026-04-02. The daily transaction count is -84.8<!--n:tx_per_day_30d_yoy_pct-->% year on year (685.0<!--n:tx_per_day_30d_k-->K a day), but most of that is one step. Between 2026-03-31 and 2026-04-02 it went from 4,169.8<!--n:l1_step_tx_before_k-->K to 1,063.4<!--n:l1_step_tx_after_k-->K, daily active contracts from 2,194.2<!--n:l1_step_contracts_before_k-->K to 184.6<!--n:l1_step_contracts_after_k-->K and daily new accounts from 137.1<!--n:l1_step_new_accounts_before_k-->K to 20.7<!--n:l1_step_new_accounts_after_k-->K, while daily active accounts barely moved (139.2<!--n:l1_step_active_accounts_before_k-->K to 137.2<!--n:l1_step_active_accounts_after_k-->K). Since the step, transactions are -18.9<!--n:tx_per_day_since_step_pct-->%. Our data cannot say whether the step is a change in what Nearblocks counts, the end of automated traffic, or real usage, so we do not read the transaction fall as a trend. Neither series is filtered for sybils or relayers, and neither says who holds or sells NEAR. Contract usage is not computed: the active-contract count is the series that steps and its definition is not documented in our sources.

**Valuation context (descriptive, not a valuation).** NEAR's market cap is 199.0<!--n:mcap_to_rev_30d_annualized_x-->× its last 30 days of captured Intents revenue annualised (510.0<!--n:mcap_to_rev_365d_x-->× on the last 365 days), at the CoinGecko snapshot of $4.82<!--n:mcap_usd_b-->B.

## 4. The supply side (pre-registered tests, with labelled extras)

Details: [`docs/supply-side.md`](docs/supply-side.md).

- **H1a fails (pre-registered).** Over the 90 days, net of deposits, stakers withdrew 6.56<!--n:h1a_ratio_as_written_x-->× what they earned. With `lst-pool.near` added the ratio is 5.96<!--n:h1a_ratio_with_lst_pool_x-->× (post hoc; the test is not redefined). The net figure includes exchange-owned stake moving, which is not a sale.
- **Where the outflow came from (exploratory; not a pre-registered test, and not reproduced from raw chain data here).** The committed file `data/raw/unstakers/account_set.json` lists 14<!--n:unstakers_a14_n--> accounts that reach one unlabelled hub within two hops. Their net unstaking over the same 90 days sums to 42.87<!--n:unstakers_a14_net_outflow_m-->M NEAR, 90.7<!--n:unstakers_a14_share_of_net_outflow_pct-->% of the pools' 47.3<!--n:pool_net_outflow_90d_m-->M NEAR net outflow (our arithmetic on that file). The extraction that produced it is not in this repository, and the repository does not record when that earlier analysis was done. By any route we could see (two hops of tracing; meta-transactions are missed), those accounts moved no NEAR into Intents.
- **H1b and H1c are inconclusive (pre-registered)** for lack of exchange labels. The buyback wallet's growth, 1.48<!--n:h1c_identified_buying_m-->M NEAR, is 33.48<!--n:h1c_ratio_lower_pct-->% of the lower bound of exchange inflow and 0.97<!--n:h1c_ratio_upper_pct-->% of the upper.
- **Rating: Hold (the frozen rule's output).** Required captured-revenue growth G_req is 33.2<!--n:rating_g_req_pct-->% a year (46.8<!--n:rating_g_req_no_hsp029_pct-->% without HSP-029); observed G_obs is 619.0<!--n:rating_g_obs_pct-->% a year in USD and 71.4<!--n:rating_g_obs_near_pct-->% in NEAR terms. Both are far above the Avoid line, 16.6<!--n:rating_g_avoid_line_pct-->% a year. G_req's constants (price, the HSP-029 ramp, its activation date, the horizon) were committed after the freeze, in the same commit as the as-written H1a result, and G_obs compares the last 180 days with the 180 before, a base window that straddles the 2026-02-23 fee switch. Neither changes the output, which H1b decides. The ladder does not feed the rule.

## Limits

- **Collector roles.** Collector A's role (the explicit buyback fee) is cross-checked: its priced inflows are 96.5<!--n:collector_a_vs_explicit_fees_pct-->% of the fees the Explorer lists. Collector B's role (a share of quote improvement) is inferred from committed, re-checkable per-swap evidence: it is paid a variable cut on 43<!--n:fee_ev_collector_b_swaps_n--> of 195<!--n:fee_ev_swaps_n--> sampled swaps across channels. The quote it would be measured against is not in the Explorer API, so the role is consistent with the docs rather than proven.
- **Partner numbers** rest on one key-gated source (the Explorer API), and the per-swap evidence is 195<!--n:fee_ev_swaps_n--> swaps from 3<!--n:fee_ev_hours_with_swaps_n--> sampled hours.
- **Escrow funding** before 2026-10-06 is sample-attributed. The identity of the escrow account and both candidate fee streams rest on NEAR's documentation, which this repository does not commit. [`docs/sources-checked.md`](docs/sources-checked.md) quotes what each page said on 2026-11-08 and marks where our wording goes beyond it; pages can change.
- **Earlier work outside this repository** is not reproduced here and, apart from the 14-account reading in section 4 (labelled exploratory), is not relied on. This repository cannot show whether that analysis preceded the freeze.
- **Rating constants.** G_req rests on assumptions committed after the freeze (section 4).
- **Usage data** are Nearblocks aggregates with undocumented definitions, not filtered for sybils or relayers, and with the 2026-04-02 step. The brief's BigQuery usage queries (daily active accounts, transaction counts, contract usage) were not run.
- **H1a** is net of deposits and includes exchange-owned stake moves.
- **H1c's numerator.** PREREG defines identified buying as the buyback wallet's balance growth, 1.48<!--n:h1c_identified_buying_m-->M NEAR over the 90 days, and H1c uses all of it. The deliveries from `intents.near` that we trace explain 0.97<!--n:bb_deliveries_90d_m-->M NEAR of it; the other 0.511<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_m-->M NEAR (34.5<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_pct-->%) is not explained by the committed data, which holds no transfers from other accounts. A read of the chain on 2026-11-08 (`docs/sources-checked.md`, 2.7; it is not in the committed data, so no figure from it is marked here) traces that remainder to ten native transfers from other accounts, almost all of it from `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, another NEAR revenue wallet, through two of that DAO's transfer proposals. So about a third of the numerator is NEAR that came from another NEAR wallet rather than from `intents.near`; where that NEAR came from, we did not trace. The verdict is inconclusive whichever numerator is used, because label coverage is below 50%.
