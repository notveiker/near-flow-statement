## F4. What funds the fee-delivery accounts (method `f7fc70c`)

- **The persistent fee accounts are funded by solvers' transfers.** For `5880ad2b…` and `143f2bb4…` the mt_transfer credits equal what they delivered (ratio 0.9999 and 0.9981) and come from solver accounts: `blockzen-1-main.near` 52.5%, `crux-solver.near` 27.4%, `solver-priv-liq.near` 12.2%. That is a flaw in the pre-registered credit definition, disclosed, not a result.
- **Reading (INFERRED).** The public fee stream is solver payments, not user-signed fees: the fee accounts are credited by the largest solvers at about the amount they later withdraw. What remains unattested is the solvers' own source, i.e. which fills these transfers correspond to.

## F7. Where the 30.8M NEAR came from (method `5ffd9f1`, 01:22:07 UTC)

- **A relay between Binance and the solver family.** `1244d552…` received 30,798,979 NEAR in 1,175 direct transfers from only four accounts and sent 30,798,978 NEAR in 1,175 transfers to `bridge-mng.near`, nothing else: coverage K = 1.0000, 100% of its outflow to one account (H-F7c supported), top 3 senders 80.8% (H-F7a supported). **All four senders are on the exchange list, as Binance proof-of-reserves wallets** (first-party list, high confidence): `451913e6…` 8.49M (316 transfers), `9c484fa5…` 8.32M (301), `d04204b6…` 8.07M (295), `5c33c621…` 5.92M (263). Exchange-list share of its inflow 100% (H-F7b supported; rule 50%). One in, one out: each Binance withdrawal is forwarded.
- **The chain therefore reads:** Binance wallets, 30.8M NEAR, relay `1244d552…`, `bridge-mng.near` wraps it, `intents.near` credits the solver family (`solver-priv-liq.near` and siblings, F1), which sent 39.2M wNEAR into the Confidential Intents escrow (Q1). In share terms: the Binance-sourced NEAR is 40.5% of all NEAR deposited through `bridge-mng.near` in the window (F2b) and, as an upper bound, 37.8% of the escrow's wNEAR inflow (30.8M of 81.5M; family inflow to the escrow was 39.2M, so about four fifths of it). Ownership of `1244d552…` itself is **INFERRED**: an account that only forwards Binance withdrawals into a solver's deposit path is the solver operator or its treasury, which the chain does not name.
- **Not established.** Whether the same NEAR then stayed in the escrow, left through `mt_burn` or native withdrawal (F1: 14.4M burned, 13.6M withdrawn natively) is not traced per unit; the 37.8% is the amount that came in, not what remained.

## F8. How much of the deposit path is exchange-sourced (method `a79e6a4`, 01:23:44 UTC)

- **Coverage 85.0%:** 54,230 of the 55,037 direct funders of `bridge-mng.near` received NEAR directly in the window, covering 64.6M of the 75.97M NEAR funded. The rest (15%) has no direct inflow in the window (funded before it, or by contract-originated transfers, which this table cannot see: the share from `intents.near` is 0 for that reason, not evidence).

Caveat: one hop only; wallets that forward exchange withdrawals through an intermediate account are counted as non-exchange, so 7.4% is a lower bound.

## F9. Do the big solvers pay the fee accounts a share of their volume? (method `2bd39cd`, 01:25:27 UTC)

- **No fixed share.** H-F9a (monthly payments / own volume within ±30% of the window ratio) is **falsified for both**. `blockzen-1-main.near` paid \$1.12M to the persistent fee accounts against \$375M of simple-swap volume, **30.0 bp**, but the monthly ratio falls 58.8, 38.0, 20.3 bp (August 96% above the window figure, October 32% below). `crux-solver.near` paid \$0.30M on \$456M, 6.6 bp, monthly 7.4, 3.3, 11.5 bp (September 50% below, October 74% above).
- **H-F9b (payments track volume) splits.** Weekly r (12 weeks): `blockzen-1-main.near` **0.58, supported**; `crux-solver.near` -0.03, **falsified**. `solver-priv-liq.near` paid \$48K in the window (not tested, see method).
- **Reading (INFERRED).** For one solver the dollars move with its volume while the rate falls as its volume grows 5 times from August to October, which is what a fixed-size payment would do, not a rate; for the other there is no relation. These are not shown to be per-fill fees: the destination is the only evidence of purpose, and the volumes are simple swaps only. The fee accounts' revenue is therefore not a clean function of solver volume.

## Deviations and corrections (disclosed)

1. **Pricing bug, found on seeing zero priced swaps in the first Q4 output.** The staging step stored each event as a string, so the diff was wrapped in a second layer of quotes and the token regex matched nothing. The synthetic self-test passed because it used a JSON-typed value. Fixed in `rb_s2_swaps.sql`, self-test extended to the production form, stages 2 and 3 and the Q3/Q4 queries rerun at 23:36 UTC. First-run outputs are kept in `data/raw/rebuild/superseded/`. No volume number from the first run was used; fill counts and solver assignment were unaffected.
2. **Q3d hit the 1,000,000-row output cap** on first run; rewritten to keep the 50 most frequent counterparties per solver and rerun.
3. **F4's credit definition counted gross `token_diff` gains as funding**, so the pre-registered reconciliation failed for two accounts and H-F4a could not be decided by its rule. The transfers-only figures are post hoc and tagged so.
4. **F5's H-F5a named the wrong denominator** (1Click instead of DefiLlama, which the repo's τ uses). The pre-registered test, which falsifies, is reported as it stands; the DefiLlama check is post hoc and tagged so.
5. The `fefundsadmin`-only series, the complex-receipt step check and the single-use round-trip note are post hoc and tagged so.
