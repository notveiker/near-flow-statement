# Chain rebuild of NEAR Intents: method, fixed before any result

Committed at the minute in the addendum heading (2026-11-12 23:30 UTC), before any query that returns chain data. Only dry runs (byte estimates), one self-test on synthetic JSON and local file builds had run. Changes no hypothesis, threshold, probability or the rating rule in PREREG.md.

**Source.** BigQuery `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us`, billed to a personal project, every query dry-run and capped (`pipeline/rebuild.py`). Archival RPC (`pipeline/rebuild_rpc.py`) for `mt_balance_of` only. Akash cannot supply this window in time (about 17M blocks; the keyless collector manages ~22 blocks/min). Raw outputs, job IDs and SHA-256 are copied to an Akash VM after the run.
**Cutoff.** Block 223,088,015 / 2026-11-04, enforced in code (every public-table scan must carry `block_height <= 223088015`; no later date may appear; result rows are checked). The node census (from block 223,963,000) is not used. `buybacks.multisignature.near`: transfers to it are counted; its balance is never read (banned tables `account_changes`, `ft_balances_daily`, `circulating_supply`; the RPC reader refuses the account).
**Windows.** Q1, Q2: 2026-07-07..2026-11-04. Q3, Q4: 2026-08-07..2026-11-04 (90 days, the span of `data/raw/explorer/daily.json`).
**Staging.** S0 flattens every `intents.near` EVENT_JSON log in the window (failed receipts, status containing FAIL, dropped) to one row per data element. S1 resolves each `mt_mint` to its originating transaction signer and receiver. S2 builds one row per `token_diff` receipt. S3 assigns the solver side. Parsing is tested on synthetic input first (`data/raw/rebuild/selftest_synthetic.json`).

## Q1. Escrow mass balance (wNEAR, `51e8f94d…7cbe`)
- **Ledger.** Daily wNEAR in and out of the escrow from `mt_mint`, `mt_burn`, `mt_transfer` and `token_diff` events where the escrow is owner, old owner, new owner or account. In: by sender (`mt_transfer` old owner; `mt_mint` transaction signer, tagged "indirect" when the transaction's receiver is not `wrap.near`). Out: by receiver. Any other event type that names the escrow is reported, not dropped.
- **Balances.** `mt_balance_of` at the last block before 2026-07-07 and at block 223,088,015. **Mass balance:** (end - start) must equal in - out. If the residual exceeds 1% of the larger of in and out, the event ledger is incomplete and every Q1 number is tagged so.
- **Classes of sender.** single-use (64-hex account with at most 2 events in the ledger); `bridge-mng.near`; named DAO (`*.sputnik-dao.near`); member of `data/raw/unstakers/account_set.json`; everyone else. Top-10 concentration of in and of out, by amount.
- **Visibility rule (stop condition).** Coverage C = public wNEAR inflow / wNEAR released. If C < 0.5 the finding is "deposits are not visible on a public ledger; the top rung cannot be audited even in principle", and attribution stops there. If C >= 0.5 the attack test runs.
- **Attack on "releases to revenue wallets are fee revenue".** Falsified if a set S of at most 10 non-user accounts (not single-use) deposits at least 80% of the window's releases to the revenue wallets by amount, AND weekly deposits from S correlate with weekly releases at r >= 0.7 at a lag of 0 to 2 days. Whether S's deposits are themselves fees is not observable and is tagged INFERRED. Release amounts are the escrow-funded deliveries in `data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv`, re-derived from chain in Q2.

## Q2. When the public fee stream moved into the escrow
- Series S1(d) = NEAR delivered by `176121bd…e3e8` to the revenue wallets (`native_withdraw` events); S2(d) = deliveries from single-use accounts (every source other than S1's account and the two collectors). Cross-check: chain totals must match `deliveries.csv` by day; differences are reported.
- **Switch day** = the single change point minimising the sum of squared errors of a two-level fit to S1 (at least 7 days each side). Significance: 10,000 permutations, seed 20261112. **Abrupt** if at least 80% of the pre/post drop falls within 3 days of the change point.
- **Replacement.** With Δ = post-mean minus pre-mean over 14-day windows around the switch day, re-routed fraction = min(1, -ΔS1 / ΔS2) when ΔS1 < 0 < ΔS2. The claim "growth on the unattested path is new revenue" is **falsified if the fraction is >= 0.5**; reported as a null if < 0.2. Also reported: the combined stream S1+S2 before and after, against the Explorer 1Click volume as control.

## Q3. Who fills the orders
- **Fill** = one `token_diff` leg. **Simple swap** = a receipt with exactly 2 legs from 2 distinct accounts. **Complex** = every other receipt: counted, never valued.
- **Solver (INFERRED)** = the participant of a simple swap with more distinct counterparties across all simple swaps in the window. Ties are unclassified. Sensitivity: the participant with more fills.
- **Reported:** share of fills and of priced volume by solver, top-1/5/10 shares, HHI, share unclassified and share of receipts that are complex. Daily series for the top 30.
- **Volume** = the largest priced token amount in the swap. Stable tokens (1Click token-list symbol containing USD or equal to DAI, snapshot price within 3% of 1; `data/raw/rebuild/tokens_1click_20261112.json`) at 1.00; BTC, ETH, SOL, ZEC, XRP, TRX, DOGE, LTC, BNB, NEAR (and their wrapped symbols, alias table in `pipeline/rebuild.py`) at that day's CoinGecko close (nearest day if missing); anything else unpriced and reported as coverage.
- **Funding of the top 20 solvers.** Inbound `mt_transfer` counterparties and `mt_mint` signers inside the window, matched against the unstakers set and the exchange list. `docs/actors.md` does not exist in this repository, so it cannot be used; this is stated, not worked around.

## Q4. The non-1Click share
- Chain daily volume (simple swaps, priced) against `data/raw/explorer/daily.json` `usd_in` (1Click) and DefiLlama `volume_near-intents`. Remainder = chain minus 1Click, INFERRED, with the share of simple swaps priced stated beside it. Chain volume is a lower bound: confidential swaps leave no public leg and complex receipts are not valued.
- Remainder split by the `referral` tag on the swap legs (INFERRED to identify the front end; near.com versus direct versus untagged).
- **DefiLlama step on 2026-09-01.** Weekly sums for 08-25..08-31 and 09-01..09-07. With L = Llama after/before and C = chain after/before: **the step shows on chain** if C >= 1 + 0.5(L - 1); **absent** if C <= 1 + 0.1(L - 1); otherwise ambiguous. Also the chain/Llama ratio before and after.

## Everything
- Numbers come from scripts reading saved files; each output file's SHA-256 and each BigQuery job ID, creation time and bytes billed go in `data/raw/rebuild/jobs.jsonl`. Nulls are reported. Anything that is a reading rather than an observation is tagged INFERRED.
- Code fixes made after this commit and before a result are disclosed in the addendum; a change after a result is disclosed with the result it changed.

## F1 (follow-up to Q1, added after the Q1 to Q4 results; fixed before it runs)
Question: Q1 found the escrow's wNEAR is mostly the inventory of a small solver family (`solver-priv-liq.near`, `solver-multichain-asset.near`, `solver-multichain-asset-escrow.near`, `solver-multichain-asset-2.near`; chosen because they are the escrow's largest counterparties in Q1 and Q3). Where does that inventory come from, and where does it go?
- **Gross recycling ratio** = wNEAR the family sent to the escrow (Q1 ledger) / the family's net external deposits (external mint deposits minus external withdrawals, window). Reported with the family's share of escrow inflow and outflow.
- **Hypothesis H-F1: the escrow is a solver liquidity pool, not a user deposit venue.** Supported if recycling ratio >= 5 and external deposits are under 25% of the family's gross inflow to the escrow. **Falsified** if external deposits (mint, by transaction signer) are at least 50% of that gross inflow.
- **Depositor concentration:** top-5 share of external deposits by signer, and whether any signer is in the unstakers set or the exchange list. A named link to staking exits or exchanges is reported either way; a signer is the initiator, not proof of ownership (INFERRED).
- Queries: `sql/rebuild/rb_f1a_family_deposits.sql`, `rb_f1b_family_withdrawals.sql`. Outputs, job IDs and hashes follow the same rules as above.

## F2 (follow-up to F1; fixed before it runs)
Question: F1 found 30.8M NEAR of wNEAR deposited into the solver family by one signer, `bridge-mng.near`. Who funds that account?
- Source: `ft_events` for `wrap.near` with `bridge-mng.near` as the affected account, window 2026-07-07..2026-11-04: inflows by counterparty and cause (`MINT` = native NEAR wrapped by the account itself, origin not visible in this table; otherwise a transfer from the counterparty), and outflows.
- **Hypothesis H-F2: the funding is concentrated and not from staking exits or exchanges.** Supported if the top 5 counterparties are at least 80% of transfer inflow and none is in the unstakers set or the exchange list. **Falsified** (as "not from staking/exchanges") if counterparties in either list supply at least 10% of inflow. If `MINT` is more than half of inflow the origin is not resolvable from this table and the question stays open (reported, not guessed).
- Output tagged INFERRED where it reads ownership from a counterparty.

## F3 (extension of Q3 and Q4 to the complex receipts; fixed before it runs)
Why: Q3 and Q4 value only simple swaps (two legs, two accounts), which leave 48.3% of receipts and 67.2% of legs out, so the chain volume is a lower bound and the solver shares are for simple swaps only. Most of the rest are 3-leg, 3-account and similar routes.
- **Anchored receipt** = a complex receipt with exactly one participant that is neither a Q3 solver (the 61) nor `intents.near`, and at least one solver participant. Its value = the largest priced token amount in the user's legs, same price rules as stage 2. Each solver leg is one fill. Unanchored receipts are counted, never valued.
- **Robustness of Q3 (H-F3a):** the top-10 share of fills over simple plus anchored-complex fills stays within 5 points of the simple-only 93.5%. If not, Q3's concentration is reported as sensitive to the receipt type.
- **Coverage and the step (H-F3b):** report the anchored volume and the share of complex receipts that are anchored. Re-apply the Q4 step rule (week after / week before 2026-09-01, thresholds as in Q4) to simple plus anchored volume. The chain-side verdict on the DefiLlama step changes only if the combined volume ratio reaches 1 + 0.5(L - 1) (L = 1.316).
- Queries: `rb_f3_s_complex.sql` (staging), `rb_f3a_daily.sql`, `rb_f3b_solver_fills.sql`.

## F2b (replaces the unresolved F2; fixed before it runs)
Why: F2 failed on table coverage, not on the question. A one-day format probe (`rb_p0_format_probe.sql`, 1.4 GB, 2026-10-01, no result about the question) showed that `wrap.near` logs for `bridge-mng.near` come in pairs of equal size, `Deposit N NEAR to bridge-mng.near` then `Transfer N from bridge-mng.near to intents.near`: the account wraps native NEAR and passes it on. The open question is where the native NEAR comes from. Change of source from F2, disclosed: legacy `wrap.near` log lines in `execution_outcomes` (about 182 GB) instead of `ft_events`.
- **Pass-through (H-F2b-a).** R = sum of `Transfer ... from bridge-mng.near to intents.near` / sum of `Deposit ... to bridge-mng.near`, window. Supported if R is in [0.95, 1.05]; **falsified** if below 0.8 or above 1.2 (then the account holds inventory or is fed wNEAR by transfer, reported by `in` and `refund` lines). Consistency with F1: the transfers to `intents.near` must be at least 30.80M NEAR (the F1 mints it signed for the solver family); if not, F1 is inconsistent and reported as such.
- **Circular funding (H-F2b-b).** Native NEAR delivered to `bridge-mng.near` by intents.near withdrawal events (`native_withdraw`, `ft_withdraw`, `mt_withdraw` with receiver `bridge-mng.near`) / sum of wrapped NEAR. Supported (the deposit path recycles intents' own withdrawals) if at least 50%; **falsified** if under 10%; between them partial. Reported with the source accounts and the top-25 receivers of `native_withdraw` for context (post hoc reading of whether `d6b77389…`, the F1 destination of the family's native withdrawals, is among them is tagged INFERRED).
- **Step 3, only if needed.** If the withdrawals explain under 80% of wrapped NEAR, the remainder is attributed with a second staged scan of `transaction_actions` (transactions whose receiver is `bridge-mng.near`, TRANSFER actions, by signer; at most 1.2 TB billed; it sees direct transfers only, not contract-originated ones, so coverage = direct transfers / residual is reported and anything under 80% coverage is called unresolved). Funders are classified against the unstakers set, the exchange list, the solver family and the escrow; H-F2b-c: top-10 funders at least 50% of the residual and none of them in the unstakers set or exchange list. Falsified if funders in either list supply 10% or more.
- Queries: `rb_f2b_s_wrap.sql` (stage), `rb_f2b_a_daily.sql`, `rb_f2b_b_withdrawals_to_bridge.sql`.

## F4 (what funds the fee-delivery accounts; fixed before it runs)
Why: Q2 measured how much NEAR reached the revenue wallets and when the source changed, but not what feeds the source accounts. Q2's own output shows that two persistent accounts, `5880ad2b…` (452.8K NEAR, 874 events) and `143f2bb4…` (194.1K, 372 events), deliver 1.37 times as much as the "public fee account" `176121bd…` (110.1K) over the window; they were treated inside the "single-use" series and not examined. Observation from the already saved Q2 output, stated before the run.
- **Credits.** wNEAR credited inside intents.near to every account that delivered native NEAR to the three wallets (`rb_f4a_fee_credits.sql`): `mt_transfer` in (by sender), `mt_mint`, positive `token_diff`. Periods: before 2026-08-19, 08-19 to 08-28, from 08-29. Groups: `5880ad2b…`, `143f2bb4…`, `176121bd…`, rest.
- **Reconciliation first.** For each group, credits / delivered NEAR. If outside [0.8, 1.5] for a group, credits are called incomplete for that group (opening balances, other tokens) and no attribution is made for it.
- **H-F4a (concentrated funding).** Largest single `mt_transfer` sender as a share of credits to `5880ad2b…` and `143f2bb4…` combined: supported at 50% or more, **falsified** below 25%.
- **H-F4b (the escrow is in the fee path).** The escrow as a sender of wNEAR credits, as a share of all credits to the groups `5880ad2b…`, `143f2bb4…` and rest, for periods mid + post: supported at 20% or more, **falsified** below 5%; before-versus-after reported. If supported, it ties Q2's partial re-routing to the Q1 escrow; if falsified, the escrow's releases are not what feeds the delivery accounts.
- Whatever channel carries most credits (swap proceeds, transfers, deposits) is reported as the answer to what feeds the accounts; ownership of any counterparty is INFERRED.

## F5 (revenue yield on chain-visible volume; fixed before it runs, committed 2026-11-13 01:18 UTC)
Why: `docs/fee-waterfall.md` states the token-claimable take τ = 4.98 bp (what the three revenue wallets receive over volume). F4 gives the delivered NEAR by day from the chain alone, and F3 gives chain-visible volume (simple plus anchored). A local computation from saved outputs, no BigQuery job; NEAR price from `aux_prices.ndjson` (CoinGecko daily, 2026-08-07 on). Window 2026-08-07..2026-11-04.
- **τ_chain** = daily delivered NEAR (Q2) × NEAR price / daily chain-visible volume (F3 combined). **τ_1c** = the same numerator over Explorer 1Click `usd_in` (`data/raw/explorer/daily.json`), the denominator the repo's τ used. Report both as window totals (sum over sum) and by calendar month; months with under 14 days are not scored.
- **H-F5a (the repo's τ replicates from the chain).** Supported if τ_1c is within ±25% of 4.98 bp; **falsified** if outside ±50%; between them reported as not replicated. The repo's window may differ from this one; that is stated in the result, and the ±25% test is applied regardless.
- **H-F5b (revenue scales with volume).** Pearson r of weekly delivered USD against weekly chain-visible volume over the 12 complete weeks: supported at r of 0.5 or more, **falsified** below 0.2. Run for the total, the persistent fee accounts and the escrow-funded group separately.
- **H-F5c (the yield is stable).** Each scored month's τ_chain within ±30% of the window τ_chain: supported; **falsified** if any scored month deviates by more than 50%.
- Whatever comes out, the unit caveat applies: the numerator is native NEAR delivered by `native_withdraw`, not all fee income (confidential-mode fees accrue inside the private ledger), so τ here is a lower bound on take (**INFERRED**).

## F6 (is DefiLlama's 2026-09-01 step in the revenue stream; fixed before it runs, committed 2026-11-13 01:20 UTC)
Why: Q4 and F3 find no step on 1 September in public swap legs (week after / week before 0.995 for simple plus anchored volume), while DefiLlama's volume rises x1.316. If that volume were ordinary flow paying the usual take, the delivered fee stream (F4/F5) would step too. Local computation on saved outputs.
- **Ratio** = delivered USD (Q2 deliveries priced at the F5 daily NEAR price) in 2026-09-01..09-07 over 2026-08-25..08-31, for the total and for the three groups of F5. Threshold as in Q4/F3: a step is mirrored if the total ratio reaches 1 + 0.5(L - 1) = 1.158 (L = 1.316); **falsified** (revenue does not mirror Llama's step) if below 1.05; between them inconclusive.
- **Placebo.** The same ratio at every other 7-day boundary from 2026-08-14 to 2026-10-27 (week after over week before), and the rank of the 09-01 ratio among them; a step is only called a step if its ratio is in the top 10% of boundaries.
- Whatever the result, the switch dates found in Q2 (08-19, 08-29) are inside the 08-25..08-31 base week for one of them; stated in the result, not adjusted for.

## F7 (where the 30.8M NEAR came from; fixed before it runs, committed 2026-11-13 01:22 UTC)
Why: F2b found one external account, `1244d552459d7230bdaf267c1d09a296282dc85251958434e670d9b9d561fac7`, sending 30,798,978 NEAR to `bridge-mng.near`, which is the whole of F1's "external" deposit into the solver family. ## F8 (how much of the deposit path is exchange-sourced; fixed before it runs, committed 2026-11-13 01:23 UTC)
Why: F7 traced the largest funder to Binance in two hops. The question for the whole deposit path is how much of the 75.97M NEAR that `bridge-mng.near` wrapped came one hop further up from exchange accounts. Direct transfers only (`transaction_actions`), window 2026-07-07..2026-11-04; label lists as in F2b (exchange list, unstakers set).
- **Per funder** (every direct funder of `bridge-mng.near`, 55,037): NEAR funded, NEAR received directly in the window, and the part of that received from exchange-list accounts, the unstakers set and `intents.near` itself.
- **Estimate (INFERRED, proportional):** exchange-sourced NEAR = sum over funders of funded NEAR × (exchange inflow / total inflow), with the ratio capped at 1; the same for the unstakers set and `intents.near`. A funder with no direct inflow in the window is `unobserved`. The proportional rule assumes a funder's outflow carries the same mix as its inflow; it is reported as an estimate, with the share of funded NEAR it covers.
- **Coverage** = funded NEAR of funders with direct inflow in the window / all funded NEAR. If under 0.6, the shares are reported for the covered part only.
- **H-F8a (exchange-sourced is large):** the estimated exchange-sourced share of the covered funded NEAR is at least 50%: supported; under 25%: **falsified**. **H-F8b (exchange-sourced excluding F7's relay):** the same share with `1244d552…` removed is at least 25%: supported; under 10%: **falsified**.
- Queries: `rb_f8_s_funder_inflow.sql` (stage), `rb_f8_a_funder_sources.sql`.

- **H-F9a (a share of volume):** for `blockzen-1-main.near` and `crux-solver.near` separately, the monthly ratio payments / own volume (months of at least 14 days) lies within ±30% of that payer's window ratio. **Falsified** if any month deviates by more than 50%.
- **H-F9b (tracks volume):** Pearson r of weekly payments against weekly own volume (12 weeks) is at least 0.5: supported; under 0.2: **falsified**.
- Whatever the result, a payment's purpose is INFERRED from the destination only.

## F10 (Intents-wide wNEAR mass balance, who it exchanges with, and where the family's native withdrawals go; fixed before it runs, committed 2026-11-13 01:29 UTC)
Why: Q1 closed the books on one account (the escrow). F2b and F7 show the deposit side. This closes them for the whole contract: does `intents.near`'s wNEAR holding on `wrap.near` equal the sum of every legacy `wrap.near` log line that moves it, and who are the counterparties besides `bridge-mng.near` (the one-day probe `rb_p2`, 1.4 GB, shows `ref-finance`, `omni.bridge.near` and unwrap lines). Second part: F1 found 13.6M NEAR of native withdrawals from the solver family going to `d6b77389…`; where does it go.
- **Balances:** `ft_balance_of(intents.near)` on `wrap.near` at block 205,869,621 (last before 2026-07-07) and at the cutoff 223,088,015 (archival RPC, `pipeline.rebuild_rpc f10`).
- **Ledger:** inflow = Transfer to `intents.near` + Refund to `intents.near`; outflow = Transfer from + Refund from `intents.near` + unwrap (`Withdraw N NEAR from intents.near`). **H-F10a (closure):** |balance change − (inflow − outflow)| / larger flow at most 1%: complete. Above 5%: the legacy-log ledger misses a movement (reported as such).
- **Counterparties:** top 10 by amount each way, with the exchange-list and unstakers-set shares of inflow and of the transfer outflow. **H-F10b (CEX exits):** exchange-list accounts receive at least 25% of the amount that leaves `intents.near` as transfers: supported; under 5%: falsified. Unwrap receivers are not on these lines (they are the `native_withdraw` events of Q2/F1).
- **`d6b77389…` (`rb_f10_s_acct_d6b7.sql`, `rb_f10_b_d6b7_flows.sql`, direct transactions only, window):** **H-F10c (it forwards to exchanges):** at least 50% of the NEAR it sends goes to exchange-list accounts: supported; under 10%: falsified. Coverage = NEAR sent / NEAR received in the window; under 0.8 is reported as incomplete.
