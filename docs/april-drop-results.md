Method and thresholds: [april-drop-method.md](april-drop-method.md), committed at `a0239ee` (2026-11-13 02:12 UTC) before any query ran. `transactions` only, filtered on `block_date` and `block_height <= 223088015`; 140 GB billed in three jobs (IDs and SHA-256 in `data/raw/april/jobs.jsonl`). Numbers from `python3 -m pipeline.april_analysis`; results in `data/derived/april_drop.json`.

## Classification, by the thresholds fixed in advance: **one class of automated traffic ended**
All four criteria hold. (i) **One signer** carries 50% of the gross drop (limit 10); two carry 80%. (ii) **71.3%** of the gross drop goes to one receiver family, `users.kaiching` (limit 60%). (iii) The signer that carries the first half of the drop falls from 29.3M transactions in the fortnight before to **479** after (-99.998%; limit -90%). (iv) The step is at 04-02: the 7-day mean after is 0.24 of the 7 days before (limit 0.60), and 04-02 is the single largest 7-day step in the series. The "broad fall" test fails (1 signer, not 1,000).

## The numbers
- **Daily series (part 1).** Transactions per day: 2026-Q1 3.49M, 2026-Q2 0.854M, a ratio of **0.245** (about a quarter, not a fifth; 2026-Q3 0.739M is 0.21 of Q1). On the step: 3,758,663 on 04-01, 1,063,375 on 04-02, 907,861 on 04-03. **Distinct signers did not fall with it:** 139,048 on 04-01, 137,183 on 04-02; Q2 over Q1 per day 0.62. **Distinct receivers collapsed:** 2,835,740 to 338,581 (Q2 over Q1 0.07). So what ended was a pattern of transactions sent to millions of different receiver accounts, not users.
- **Fortnights (part 3).** Transactions 51.83M before, 13.03M after (0.25), net change -38.8M, gross signer drop 40.4M carried by 169,382 signers with a decline, out of 350,523. Signers for 50% / 80% / 90% of it: 1 / 2 / 2.

By receiver family: `users.kaiching` (receivers named `<something>.users.kaiching`) 28.8M to 1.7K (71.3%); 64-hex implicit accounts 9.5M to 1.8M (21.6%); `wallet.kaiching` 2.1M to 462 (5.2%). The signers that did not fall: `relay.tg` 1.88M to 1.45M, `0-relay.hot.tg` 1.89M to 1.60M.
- **Concentration by month (part 2).** The top 20 receivers hold 20% to 25% of transactions each month from 2025-07 to 2026-03 and **56% to 78% from 2026-04**; the top 5 hold 14% to 21% before and 38% to 50% after. ## What this says and does not
- The fall is mostly one signer's traffic stopping on 2026-04-02, plus `sweat-migration.near` and a third, `users.kaiching`: in the fortnight, removing those three signers leaves 13.02M transactions before and 12.88M after (0.99), against 51.83M and 13.03M with them. Whether the traffic stopped, was batched or migrated is not visible here (**INFERRED** only that it is automated: one account sending 29M transactions in 14 days to millions of receiver accounts). The account names are not labelled to any product in this repository.
- **Implication for any "activity" series built on raw transaction counts** (including this repository's history-sample `tx_per_day` and `signers_per_100`): the quarter-on-quarter fall in 2026 is driven by this one class, so ratios across 2026-04 are not comparable unless it is removed.
- Signers are accounts, not people; a family is a naming rule; failed transactions are included.
