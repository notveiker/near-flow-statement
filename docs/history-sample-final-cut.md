## Final cut
- **The pre-registered stopping rule** (amended 2026-11-11, `docs/history-sample-prereg.md`): stop each stratum after round 6 (before 2025-Q1) or round 20 (2025-Q1 on), **or at 2026-11-12 12:00 UTC, whichever comes first; the analysis uses every cluster sealed by then**.
- **Collection did not stop at 12:00 UTC.** It ran to the round caps: the last cluster was sealed **2026-11-13 01:26:11 UTC**, 13 h 26 min after the stop time. **198 clusters were sealed by the stop time and 70 after** (all 70 in 2025-Q1 to 2026-Q4, 8 to 9 per stratum; pre-2025 strata were complete at 6 each).
- **Final cut by the rule as written: the 198 clusters sealed by 2026-11-12 12:00 UTC** (19,800 blocks less null heights; 6 per stratum before 2025, 11 or 12 per stratum from 2025-Q1; list in `final_cut.json`, flagged in `seal_list.tsv`). The 70 later clusters are independent uniform draws from the same strata, so they are a valid extension of the sample by the design's own argument (the first k of a stratum are uniform), but using them is not what the stopping rule says.
- **The existing preliminary results (`data/derived/history_results.json`, `preliminary: true`) use 268 clusters, not the 198.** Interim results were also computed and written up while collection was still running (the `history_honesty_150/160/164` and progress files), so the decision to let collection continue past the stop time was not made blind. **Nothing has been recomputed for either cut, and no U-test is re-scored here.** The scoring run should report the 198-cluster cut as the pre-registered result and the 268-cluster set as a labelled extension, with the verdicts shown for both.

## Sampler validation
- **The design's validation has not been run.** `history-sample-prereg.md` ("Validation of the sampler itself") calls for drawing the same kind of clusters 200 times from our own node census of a window of recent days and reporting how often the 90% interval covers the census value for each metric.
- **Why not.** The repository holds only aggregates of the census (`census_usage.json`, `census_hourly.json`: 10.3 hours, blocks 223,963,000 to 224,027,999 (64,992 blocks), 343K transactions), not its blocks. Drawing clusters from it needs the raw blocks, which were on the node host (`/data/near/archive`) and are not on this Mac or the sampler VM. Re-fetching them from NEARDATA at the rate we saw (about 13 blocks a minute) would take about 83 hours. The census window is also inside the forward-test window.
- **What would make it possible:** the node's `/data/near/archive` (or a copy), or a smaller census window pulled from NEARDATA. Until then the intervals in the history report cannot be called validated, and the report should say so.
