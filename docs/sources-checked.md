## 3. The escrow re-check: what it tests and what it does not

**What `verify` tests for each row** (`pipeline/fetch_escrow.py`, functions `check_delivery` and `check_funding`). The delivery transaction must contain a successful NEAR transfer from `intents.near` to the row's wallet of exactly `amount_yocto`, executed at the row's `block_height`, and a `native_withdraw` event for the same wallet and amount whose `account_id` is the expected source: collector A or B for those paths, the row's `single_use_account`, or the account named after `recurring:` in `path_detail`. If the row has a `funding_tx_hash`, that transaction must contain a `dip4` wNEAR `transfer` to the single-use account from the row's `funder`, of exactly `amount_yocto` when `exact_amount_flag` is 1, crediting the recorded `funding_receipt_id`; and a row whose `path` is `escrow_unshield` must have the treasury as `funder`.

**What it does not test.** It compares the table with the chain; it does not test how the table's columns were assigned. `data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv` has no writer in `pipeline/`: the code only reads it. What the file itself shows:

- `path` and `path_detail`. Collector A and B rows are deliveries whose withdrawing account is the fixed collector id. `recurring:<account>` rows name two recurring accounts (`176121bd…`, and one other account that delivered once to `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`). The rows with a `single_use_account` (2,180) each name a withdrawing account that appears in exactly one row of the table (2,180 distinct accounts).
- `funder`, `funding_tx_hash`, `funding_receipt_id`, `exact_amount_flag`, `funding_evidence`, `attribution`. Filled for the 1,051<!--n:escrow_recheck_funding_hops_n--> rows whose funding transfer was looked up. `funding_evidence` says where it came from: a BigQuery scan of the 30-day window (every single-use row in it; the job id `bqjob_r275b038b73d7c093_000001a1af3708a8_1` is recorded in `data/MANIFEST.json` and printed on the dashboard's ladder card, and its hex time field decodes to 2026-11-06 04:56:21 UTC, an inference from the id's format) or three labelled FastNEAR samples (earlier rows, and some window rows that carry both labels). The other 1,129 single-use rows, all before 2026-10-06, have no funding transfer in the table (`attribution` is `sample`); the re-check can test their deliveries but not their funding.
- The label appears to follow the funder wherever a funding transfer was looked up: one single-use account (2026-08-11) was funded by an account other than the treasury and is labelled `other` / `single_use_nontreasury`, not `escrow_unshield`. The code that assigned the labels is not in the repository, so this is an inference from the file.
- `exact_amount_flag` is 1 on all 1,051<!--n:escrow_recheck_funding_hops_n--> rows that have a funding transfer; no row carries 0. `check_funding` has a branch for 0, but no row exercises it, so the file cannot show a funding transfer that was not for the exact amount. Whether the scan recorded such cases, or only matches, cannot be told without its SQL.
- The SQL of that October funding scan is not committed. `sql/buyback_withdraw_sources.sql` is a different query: it returns deliveries by day, withdrawing account and wallet, and looks up no funding.

**What this means for the thesis sentence "all [N] such deliveries were funded by the escrow for the exact amount, to the yoctoNEAR ([P]%)"** (where [N] and [P] stand for the metrics `escrow_funded_exact_30d_n` and `escrow_funded_exact_30d_pct`). `escrow_ladder` in pipeline/compute.py tallies the table's `funder` and `exact_amount_flag` columns over the window's rows that have a single-use account. Three things bear on how much that tally shows. (1) The set it is taken over does not depend on those columns. In the committed BigQuery output (`data/raw/bq/buyback_withdraw_sources.json`, whose SQL is committed and looks up no funding), the window's withdrawing accounts other than collector A, collector B and `176121bd…` number 909<!--n:escrow_single_use_30d_n-->, each appears once, and they are the same accounts as the table's single-use accounts (a recount from the two files). A funder other than the treasury would have lowered the figure, and one such row exists, before the window (2026-08-11, labelled `other`). (2) Each of the window's 909<!--n:escrow_recheck_window_funding_ok_n--> recorded funding transfers was re-read from the chain by `verify --all` and found as recorded (funder, receiving account, exact amount and receipt id); two of the 909<!--n:escrow_recheck_window_single_use_n--> deliveries fail only on block height. (3) Not testable from the repository: the scan that found the funding transfers, since its SQL is absent, and the code that assigned the other labels. Before 2026-10-06 the funding hops are sampled: the 1,129 earlier single-use rows carry no funding transfer in the table, and the labels of the earlier rows rest on the sampled hops. Also independent of the table's labelling: the delivery list matches the chain (2.6, 2.7).
