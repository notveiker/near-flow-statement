# near-flow-statement

A pre-registered, re-runnable token flow statement for NEAR (issuance, staking withdrawals, exchange inflows, identified buying) and the investment thesis built on it, for NEAR HACKATHON 2026, track Investment Thesis.

**Who buys and who sells NEAR?** The pre-registered question is its market-wide form: how much NEAR actually flows to exchanges, and how much of that do identified buyers absorb? (It makes no claim about which coins are sold.) The question the evidence can answer is how much of NEAR's value capture can be verified.

> Status, 2026-11-13: the offline rebuild passes. H1a, H1b and H1c have results. The forward tests F1-F4 are pending until 2026-11-14. This is research, not financial advice.

**In short.** Terms are defined in [`docs/glossary.md`](docs/glossary.md). Pre-registered result: H1a (most staking rewards stay staked) fails; H1b (less NEAR reaches exchanges than is minted) and H1c (identified buying is at least 25% of net exchange inflow) are inconclusive; and the frozen rating rule outputs Hold, which carries no view on NEAR. Exploratory, chosen after the freeze: over 30 days the NEAR that verifiably reached the three wallets DefiLlama counts as NEAR Intents revenue was 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance, of which 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points trace to public fee collectors (3.97<!--n:ladder_collectors_public_swap_pct_of_issuance--> from publicly settled swaps, 0.56<!--n:ladder_collectors_escrow_funded_pct_of_issuance--> from fees paid by escrow-funded accounts), 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> arrived as releases from an escrow whose fee stream is unattested (11.81<!--n:ladder_escrow_all_pct_of_issuance--> passed through the escrow in all), and 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> came from one other source. So, if those three are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets, the value capture that can be verified is a range, not a number. This is not a valuation and not advice. [`THESIS.md`](THESIS.md) opens with a five-point bottom line.

## NEAR integration

This project reads NEAR and deploys nothing on it: no contract, and no code here signs or sends a transaction. What it reads:

- the NEAR BigQuery public dataset `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us`, through query results committed in `data/raw/`;
- FastNEAR's archival RPC and transaction API, and the near.org archival RPC as the scorer's fallback;
- the NEAR Intents Explorer API, which needs a partner key (the repository commits daily aggregates and a sample of swaps);
- the on-chain accounts named in `THESIS.md`: `intents.near`, the Confidential Intents escrow, the fee collectors and the three revenue wallets.

The subject is NEAR's token flows: issuance, staking withdrawals, exchange inflows, and the NEAR that Intents fees deliver to NEAR's wallets. Prices, revenue and usage series come from CoinGecko, DefiLlama and Nearblocks (see "Data sources and attribution").

## Summary

Same order and caveats as [`THESIS.md`](THESIS.md): the pre-registered result first, then the exploratory one.

**Pre-registered scorecard.** [`PREREG.md`](PREREG.md) was frozen at commit `413d776` (2026-11-05 22:53:29 UTC), two minutes before the first BigQuery result the repository records (22:55:37 UTC in `data/MANIFEST.json`). Commit and manifest times are written by the author's machine; one external record, GitHub's activity log, dates the push of the freeze commit to 2026-11-05 22:53:33 UTC (see "Integrity rules"). Window: the 90 days 2026-08-07 to 2026-11-04.

- **H1a fails.** The hypothesis: most staking rewards stay staked. Net staking outflow was 6.56<!--n:h1a_ratio_as_written_x-->× staking rewards, and a pass needed under 0.5×. The figure is net of deposits and includes exchange-owned stake moving, which is not a sale. It nets large principal flows in both directions, so it measures net principal churn, not whether rewards themselves stay staked.
- **H1b and H1c are inconclusive.** H1b: less NEAR reaches exchanges than is minted. H1c: identified buying is at least 25% of net exchange inflow. Only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 NEAR receivers carry an exchange label (10.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_tier1_count_pct-->% a cited one; the rest are labelled by our own sweep rule), below the 50% the pre-registration requires. So net exchange inflow is a range, 4.42<!--n:h1b_net_inflow_lower_m-->M to 152.45<!--n:h1b_net_inflow_upper_m-->M NEAR, against 8.0098<!--n:h1a_gross_issuance_90d_m-->M NEAR minted.
- **The frozen rating rule outputs Hold.** That carries no view on NEAR: below 50% label coverage H1b can come out pass or inconclusive but never fail, and observed revenue growth (619.0<!--n:rating_g_obs_pct-->% a year; 71.4<!--n:rating_g_obs_near_pct-->% in NEAR terms) is far above the Avoid line (16.6<!--n:rating_g_avoid_line_pct-->% a year), so the rule could only ever have output Buy or Hold; had H1b failed it would also have been Hold. That growth is a step in the share of fees NEAR captures, not growth in activity ([`docs/second-review.md`](docs/second-review.md), section 6).

**Exploratory, chosen after the freeze (not pre-registered).** Over the 30 days to 2026-11-04, the NEAR delivered to the three wallets DefiLlama counts as NEAR Intents revenue (`buybacks.multisignature.near`, `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`) equalled 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance (88,295<!--n:net_issuance_near_per_day_window--> NEAR a day). Only 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points of that trace on chain to public fee collectors, and of those, 3.97<!--n:ladder_collectors_public_swap_pct_of_issuance--> come from publicly settled swaps and 0.56<!--n:ladder_collectors_escrow_funded_pct_of_issuance--> from fees paid by accounts the escrow had funded ([`docs/collector-funders.md`](docs/collector-funders.md)). Another 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> points arrive as unshields from the Confidential Intents escrow (`THESIS.md`, section 2, shows how it is identified), and the fee stream behind them is unattested. The last 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> points come from one other recurring source. So, if those three are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets (DefiLlama's adapter lists these three, taken from NEAR's own Dune query, so its revenue is the same count, not an independent check), the value capture that can be verified is a range, 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->% to 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance. A per-stream attestation by NEAR of which fees fund which escrow releases would turn the range into one number. No valuation is made, and the ladder does not feed the rating rule. It was developed alongside the pre-registered tests: a first split of the buyback wallet's inflows into collectors and single-use accounts dates from 2026-11-06 03:00 UTC (`33d7ec7`), its code was first committed in `51e396b` (2026-11-06 23:19:20 UTC), before the H1b/H1c and H1a-as-written results were committed, and it became the thesis headline on 2026-11-07 (00:21 UTC). The addendum's 2026-11-08 entry gives the times.

**Forward tests F1-F4 are pending.** The predictions are fixed in `PREREG.md`. The window is 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13. They are scored after 2026-11-14 00:00 UTC by `python3 -m pipeline.score_forward --score`, which refuses a live run earlier (exit status 2, no network call; the offline `--from-snapshot` mode is described in the optional table below). Every result will be published, misses included. The scorer was written after the window opened, which the addendum discloses. The first dry run of the scorer read no scoring-window day for F2 to F4 and only the window-opening block for F1 (item 7 of the 2026-11-06 23:49 UTC entry of [`PREREG-ADDENDUM.md`](PREREG-ADDENDUM.md)); later dry runs, and direct reads of the buyback wallet's balance, did touch window days and are disclosed in its 2026-11-08 05:04 UTC entry.

**Longer-dated predictions P1-P3 are pending.** `PREREG.md` also pre-registers three longer-dated predictions (the Intents share of the peer set, 90-day captured revenue, and captured revenue against the value of new supply). They resolve on 2027-05-04 and 2027-11-04 and cannot be scored yet; the dashboard lists them as not computed here.

**What this does not show.**

- Who sold. NEAR is fungible, so every flow result is a market-wide balance, not an attribution to sellers.
- How much NEAR reached exchanges. Only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 receivers carry an exchange label (10.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_tier1_count_pct-->% a cited one), and no citable NEAR address was found, in a time-boxed search, for OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, HTX, Crypto.com, Upbit or Bithumb (OKX publishes a proof-of-reserves download, but no machine-readable NEAR list was reachable).
- Which fee stream funds the escrow releases. Only NEAR can attest that.
- ETF and treasury-company holdings. `PREREG.md` says they would be reported where they can be measured; none was measured, and none is reported.
- Where all of the buyback wallet's growth came from. H1c's numerator is that wallet's balance growth, 1.48<!--n:h1c_identified_buying_m-->M NEAR over the 90 days. The deliveries from `intents.near` that we trace explain 0.97<!--n:bb_deliveries_90d_m-->M NEAR of it; the other 0.511<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_m-->M NEAR (34.5<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_pct-->%) is not explained by the committed data. The H1c verdict does not depend on it.
- Why daily transactions fell by most of their level between 2026-03-31 and 2026-04-02. The usage series are Nearblocks aggregates, not BigQuery. Our data cannot say whether that step is a change in what Nearblocks counts, the end of automated traffic or real usage, and contract usage is not computed (`THESIS.md`, section 3).
- Partner and channel numbers rest on one key-gated source, the Explorer API. The repository commits its daily aggregates and a sample of swaps, not every swap.

## Optional: needs the network, a key or gcloud

Nothing here is needed to check the results. These commands refresh or re-check the committed snapshots against the live sources. Run them in a throwaway clone. Several overwrite files in `data/raw/` and `data/MANIFEST.json`. The two no-key on-chain re-checks (`fetch_escrow verify --sample 60` and `fetch_fee_evidence trace`) reproduce the committed data byte for byte, but each still rewrites its entry in `data/MANIFEST.json` (a new `fetched_at` and a different `source` text). Eleven committed inputs have no command in this repository that regenerates them, so no command below refreshes them: the per-delivery table behind the ladder (`data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv`; `fetch_escrow` re-checks its rows against the chain but never writes it), the exchange-label list and the Binance snapshot behind it (`data/raw/labels/`, two files), four small RPC files that feed H1a, H1c and the rating rule (`data/raw/rpc/buyback_balance_endpoints.json`, `buyback_wnear_endpoints.json`, `pool_endpoints_archival.json` and `rating_assumptions.json`) and the four files in `data/raw/unstakers/`. ## Integrity rules

- **The pre-registration is frozen.** `PREREG.md` sits at git tag `prereg-freeze` (commit `413d776`) with SHA-256 `19e470be5398f67ce7b6b5a51f007c0c7a0fcc059b61853c52caf7401452e240`. It is never edited. Step 7 checks all three. The tag and the commit have the same timestamp, so the tag adds no independent evidence of timing.
- **The addendum is a dated record, and append-only from 2026-11-08.** `PREREG-ADDENDUM.md` records, as dated entries, the post-freeze results, method choices and corrections for the pre-registered work, including how its frozen definitions were read and implemented. `PREREG.md` itself is never edited. The addendum was described as append-only before 2026-11-08, but five commits (`e0edac8`, `8e59a3f`, `5188f5a`, `01187d5`, `15f2859`) changed nine existing lines; its 2026-11-08 entry lists them, and from that entry on the text above it is pinned by SHA-256 in `tests/test_integrity.py`, so an edit of it fails the tests. Corrections made in other files before 2026-11-08 are listed in the same entry where git shows them; the full record of every change is `git log`.
- **Numbers in prose are machine-checked, but only where they are marked.** A number that exists in `data/metrics.json` carries a `<!--n:…-->` marker, and `python3 -m pipeline.check --numbers` fails if the marked number differs from the metric (step 3). It reads `README.md`, `THESIS.md`, `PREREG-ADDENDUM.md` and `docs/*.md`. It cannot find a metric number that has no marker, so keeping the markers complete is the writers' job: mark every figure you take from `data/metrics.json`. The dashboard's numbers are tied to their sources by `tests/test_render.py`.
- **Raw snapshots are hash-checked.** `data/MANIFEST.json` records the SHA-256 of every file in `data/raw/`, and `python3 -m pipeline.check --manifest` fails on any mismatch, missing file or unlisted file (step 1). Eleven inputs have no regenerating command here (see the optional table).
- **Pre-registered and exploratory results are labelled.** The ladder is exploratory (chosen after the freeze) and does not feed the rating rule. It was developed alongside the pre-registered tests, not after them: a first split of the buyback wallet's inflows into collectors and single-use accounts was committed in `33d7ec7` (2026-11-06 03:00:11 UTC), its code and four of its five metrics in `51e396b` (2026-11-06 23:19:20 UTC), before `1c032a0` (the H1b and H1c results, 23:23:40) and `9ee9082` (H1a as written, 23:49:26), and `THESIS.md` was first committed with the ladder as its headline in `3bf67c3` (2026-11-07 00:21:24 UTC). The addendum records this (entry 2026-11-08, which qualifies its 2026-11-07 00:21 UTC entry), and `THESIS.md` labels the ladder wherever it appears. `THESIS.md` says at the top that only the tests in its section 4, the forward tests F1-F4 and the longer-dated predictions P1-P3 are pre-registered, and that section 4's additions are labelled where they appear.
- **Forward tests are scored by a script that refuses a live run before 2026-11-14 00:00 UTC.** `pipeline/score_forward.py` scores F1-F4 as `PREREG.md` words them, with the operational readings listed in its docstring and in the addendum (2026-11-08 02:03 UTC entry, item 7). Its `--from-snapshot` mode re-scores a saved snapshot offline and does not compare the clock with the cutoff, and it believes the snapshot's own recorded times (see the optional table), so a score file is evidence together with its snapshot and the git history. The script was written after the forward window opened, which the addendum discloses. Its first dry run read no scoring-window day for F2 to F4 and only the window-opening block for F1 (item 7 of the 2026-11-06 23:49 UTC entry); later dry runs, and direct reads of the buyback wallet's balance, did touch window days and are disclosed in the 2026-11-08 05:04 UTC entry. A test with missing data is `not scorable`, never estimated. Every result will be published, misses included. Output of its `--dry-run` mode is never a score.
- **What these checks do not show.** Git timestamps are written by the committing machine, and so are the times in `data/MANIFEST.json`; apart from the GitHub record below, nothing external attests either. `PREREG.md` says its hash "is published on NEAR mainnet (see `docs/ANCHOR.md` once anchored)"; no `docs/ANCHOR.md` is committed, so there is no on-chain anchor to check here. BigQuery job IDs, which embed their creation time, are recorded only for the H1b/H1c runs and the October escrow scan. The H1a queries have none, so for H1a the order of events rests on commit and manifest times alone. One external record does exist: GitHub's activity list dates the push of the freeze commit to 2026-11-05 22:53:33 UTC (`PREREG-ADDENDUM.md`, 2026-11-12 23:50 UTC entry, item 2).
