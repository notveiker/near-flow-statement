# near-flow-statement

An investment thesis for NEAR that anyone can re-run, built for NEAR HACKATHON 2026 (Investment Thesis track). It has three parts: a token flow statement for NEAR (what is minted, what leaves staking, what reaches exchanges, what Intents fees send back), a static dashboard, and a model of what NEAR's price assumes out to 2031. Research, not financial advice.

**The problem.** NEAR issues about 88,295<!--n:net_issuance_near_per_day_window--> new NEAR a day, net of burns. NEAR Intents earns fees, and some of them reach NEAR's revenue wallets as NEAR. Whether those fees can grow into that dilution, and whether the price already assumes they will, is the long-term question for the token. A headline revenue number does not answer it. This repository measures both sides from public data, and every marked number in it is recomputed offline by one command.

**What we found.** Points 1 to 3 are exploratory, chosen after the pre-registration was frozen. Point 4 is pre-registered.

1. **What reaches NEAR today.** Over the 30 days to 2026-11-04, the NEAR delivered to the three wallets DefiLlama counts as NEAR Intents revenue equalled 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance. Only 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points of that trace on chain to public fee collectors. Another 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> arrived as releases from the Confidential Intents escrow, whose fee stream is unattested, and 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> came from one other source. If those three are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets, the value capture that can be verified is a range, not a number.
2. **What the price assumes.** If Intents' captured revenue carried NEAR's price alone, it would have to grow 84.9<!--n:ic_implied_cagr_r15_x25_pct-->% a year to 2031 (for the price to rise 15<!--n:ic_base_r_pct-->% a year and end at 25<!--n:ic_base_x-->× revenue). That is faster than the cross-chain venues tracked here have grown over any window in the data. Their 13.4<!--n:ic_market_cagr_1y_pct-->% growth last year was all Intents' own: the others changed by -53.4<!--n:ic_market_ex_intents_cagr_1y_pct-->%. At that pace, and with Intents at its best share, NEAR would have to capture 2.5<!--n:ic_take_needed_vs_gross_x-->× Intents' whole gross fee.
3. **Where that leaves the case.** In the base case, captured revenue would pay for 44.6<!--n:ic_base_coverage_2031_pct-->% of NEAR's dilution in 2031, and only the most favourable combinations in the data reach what the price would assume. Intents has had the largest share of its market over the year to 2026-11-04, but as a claim on Intents' fees NEAR is priced for more growth than the data show at today's fees. The same code on data to 2026-11-13 gives 79.8<!--n:upd_ic_implied_cagr_r15_x25_pct-->% a year, 1.6<!--n:upd_ic_take_needed_vs_gross_x-->× and 62.2<!--n:upd_ic_base_coverage_2031_pct-->%. The gap narrowed because NEAR's market cap fell to $4.48<!--n:upd_mcap_usd_b-->B and 90-day captured revenue rose to $21.2<!--n:upd_ic_rev_90d_ann_m-->M at an annual rate. Intents' 30-day share of the peer set fell to 45.8<!--n:upd_intents_share_peerset_30d_pct-->% as THORChain's rose to 37.6<!--n:upd_thorchain_share_peerset_30d_pct-->%; Intents still leads over 30, 90 and 365 days.
4. **How our predictions scored.** Three hypotheses, a rating rule and seven dated forecasts were frozen before the first BigQuery result the repository records, and every result is published. The frozen question was how much NEAR actually flows to exchanges, and how much of that identified buyers absorb (it makes no claim about which coins are sold). H1a predicted that most staking rewards stay staked, which needed net staking outflow under 0.5× staking rewards; it was 6.56<!--n:h1a_ratio_as_written_x-->×. H1b and H1c could not be settled either way, because only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 NEAR receivers carry an exchange label. So the frozen rating rule gives no buy or avoid signal, which carries no view on NEAR. Of the four forward tests, scored on 2026-11-14 at 07:02 UTC, F1 and F2 came true and F3 and F4 did not.

The method, the scenarios, the peers, the Monte Carlo (and why the thesis does not rest on it) and the signposts are in [`THESIS.md`](THESIS.md) section 6, [`docs/investment-case.md`](docs/investment-case.md) and the dashboard's Investment case tab. Terms are defined in [`docs/glossary.md`](docs/glossary.md). The pre-registered tests, the value-capture ladder and the 1Click charts stay at 2026-11-04, because their inputs need a BigQuery scan or the Explorer API key; the update to 2026-11-13 (`pipeline/update.py`, exploratory, chosen on 2026-11-14 after F1-F4 were scored) re-fetches DefiLlama, CoinGecko and Nearblocks only.

## NEAR integration

This project reads NEAR and deploys nothing on it: no contract, and no code here signs or sends a transaction. What it reads:

- the NEAR BigQuery public dataset `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us`, through query results committed in `data/raw/`;
- FastNEAR's archival RPC and transaction API, and the near.org archival RPC as the scorer's fallback;
- the NEAR Intents Explorer API, which needs a partner key (the repository commits daily aggregates and a sample of swaps);
- the on-chain accounts named in `THESIS.md`: `intents.near`, the Confidential Intents escrow, the fee collectors and the three revenue wallets.

The subject is NEAR's token flows: issuance, staking withdrawals, exchange inflows, and the NEAR that Intents fees deliver to NEAR's wallets. Prices, revenue and usage series come from CoinGecko, DefiLlama and Nearblocks (see "Data sources and attribution").

## Summary

The pre-registered results first, then the exploratory ones, with the caveats [`THESIS.md`](THESIS.md) gives.

**Pre-registered scorecard.** [`PREREG.md`](PREREG.md) was frozen at commit `413d776` (2026-11-05 22:53:29 UTC), two minutes before the first BigQuery result the repository records (22:55:37 UTC in `data/MANIFEST.json`). Commit and manifest times are written by the author's machine; one external record, GitHub's activity log, dates the push of the freeze commit to 2026-11-05 22:53:33 UTC (see "Integrity rules"). Window: the 90 days 2026-08-07 to 2026-11-04.

- **H1a: more NEAR left staking than rewards added.** The hypothesis: most staking rewards stay staked, which needed net staking outflow under 0.5× staking rewards. It was 6.56<!--n:h1a_ratio_as_written_x-->×. The figure is net of deposits and includes exchange-owned stake moving, which is not a sale. It nets large principal flows in both directions, so it measures net principal churn, not whether rewards themselves stay staked.
- **H1b and H1c: ranges too wide to settle.** H1b: less NEAR reaches exchanges than is minted. H1c: identified buying is at least 25% of net exchange inflow. Only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 NEAR receivers carry an exchange label (10.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_tier1_count_pct-->% a cited one; the rest are labelled by our own sweep rule), below the 50% the pre-registration requires. So net exchange inflow is a range, 4.42<!--n:h1b_net_inflow_lower_m-->M to 152.45<!--n:h1b_net_inflow_upper_m-->M NEAR, on either side of the 8.0098<!--n:h1a_gross_issuance_90d_m-->M NEAR minted, and identified buying is 33.48<!--n:h1c_ratio_lower_pct-->% of its lower bound but 0.97<!--n:h1c_ratio_upper_pct-->% of its upper.
- **The frozen rating rule gives no buy or avoid signal.** That carries no view on NEAR: below 50% label coverage H1b can come out confirmed or unsettled but never refuted, and observed revenue growth (619.0<!--n:rating_g_obs_pct-->% a year; 71.4<!--n:rating_g_obs_near_pct-->% in NEAR terms) is far above the avoid line (16.6<!--n:rating_g_avoid_line_pct-->% a year), so the rule could only ever have given a buy signal or none; had H1b been refuted it would still have given none. That growth is a step in the share of fees NEAR captures, not growth in activity ([`docs/second-review.md`](docs/second-review.md), section 6).

**Exploratory, chosen after the freeze (not pre-registered).** Over the 30 days to 2026-11-04, the NEAR delivered to the three wallets DefiLlama counts as NEAR Intents revenue (`buybacks.multisignature.near`, `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`) equalled 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance (88,295<!--n:net_issuance_near_per_day_window--> NEAR a day). Only 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance--> points of that trace on chain to public fee collectors, and of those, 3.97<!--n:ladder_collectors_public_swap_pct_of_issuance--> come from publicly settled swaps and 0.56<!--n:ladder_collectors_escrow_funded_pct_of_issuance--> from fees paid by accounts the escrow had funded ([`docs/collector-funders.md`](docs/collector-funders.md)). Another 11.25<!--n:ladder_escrow_unshields_pct_of_issuance--> points arrive as unshields from the Confidential Intents escrow (`THESIS.md`, section 2, shows how it is identified), and the fee stream behind them is unattested. The last 0.03<!--n:ladder_other_pct_of_issuance--> points come from one other recurring source. So, if those three are all of NEAR's Intents revenue wallets (DefiLlama's adapter lists these three, taken from NEAR's own Dune query, so its revenue is the same count, not an independent check), the value capture that can be verified is a range, 4.53<!--n:ladder_public_collectors_pct_of_issuance-->% to 15.81<!--n:ladder_all_wallets_pct_of_issuance-->% of net issuance. A per-stream attestation by NEAR of which fees fund which escrow releases would turn the range into one number. No valuation is made, and the ladder does not feed the rating rule. It was developed alongside the pre-registered tests: a first split of the buyback wallet's inflows into collectors and single-use accounts dates from 2026-11-06 03:00 UTC (`33d7ec7`), its code was first committed in `51e396b` (2026-11-06 23:19:20 UTC), before the H1b/H1c and H1a-as-written results were committed, and it became the thesis headline on 2026-11-07 (00:21 UTC). The addendum's 2026-11-08 entry gives the times.

**Forward tests F1-F4, scored 2026-11-14 07:02 UTC: two passed, two missed.** F1 passed (the buyback wallet held 2,279,854 NEAR, inside [2.21M, 2.40M]), F2 passed (86,208 NEAR a day, inside [86.0K, 89.5K]), F3 missed (a captured take of 4.22 bp, where the prediction was below 4.0 bp) and F4 missed (NEAR Intents was 37.2% of the peer set's volume; THORChain led the week). The Brier score is 0.266. The result is `data/forward/score_20261114T070207Z/result.json`, and `THESIS.md` has the table under "How our predictions scored". The predictions are fixed in `PREREG.md`. The window is 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13. They were scored by `python3 -m pipeline.score_forward --score`, which refuses a live run before 2026-11-14 00:00 UTC (exit status 2, no network call; the offline `--from-snapshot` mode is described in the optional table below). Every result is published, misses included. The scorer was written after the window opened, which the addendum discloses. The first dry run of the scorer read no scoring-window day for F2 to F4 and only the window-opening block for F1 (item 7 of the 2026-11-06 23:49 UTC entry of [`PREREG-ADDENDUM.md`](PREREG-ADDENDUM.md)); later dry runs, and direct reads of the buyback wallet's balance, did touch window days and are disclosed in its 2026-11-08 05:04 UTC entry.

**Longer-dated predictions P1-P3 resolve in 2027.** `PREREG.md` also pre-registers three longer-dated predictions (the Intents share of the peer set, 90-day captured revenue, and captured revenue against the value of new supply). They resolve on 2027-05-04 and 2027-11-04 and cannot be scored yet; the dashboard lists them as not computed here.

**What this does not show.**

- Who sold. NEAR is fungible, so every flow result is a market-wide balance, not an attribution to sellers.
- How much NEAR reached exchanges. Only 20.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_count_pct-->% of the top-100 receivers carry an exchange label (10.0<!--n:h1b_coverage_tier1_count_pct-->% a cited one), and no citable NEAR address was found, in a time-boxed search, for OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, HTX, Crypto.com, Upbit or Bithumb (OKX publishes a proof-of-reserves download, but no machine-readable NEAR list was reachable).
- Which fee stream funds the escrow releases. Only NEAR can attest that. The chain shows that the releases move day by day with confidential swap volume, as fees on those swaps would (`docs/confidential-flow.md`, exploratory).
- ETF and treasury-company holdings. `PREREG.md` says they would be reported where they can be measured; none was measured, and none is reported.
- Where all of the buyback wallet's growth came from. H1c's numerator is that wallet's balance growth, 1.48<!--n:h1c_identified_buying_m-->M NEAR over the 90 days. The deliveries from `intents.near` that we trace explain 0.97<!--n:bb_deliveries_90d_m-->M NEAR of it; the other 0.511<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_m-->M NEAR (34.5<!--n:bb_growth_not_delivered_pct-->%) is not explained by the committed data; reads of the chain not committed here trace it to an earlier transfer between NEAR's own revenue wallets (`THESIS.md`, Limits, "H1c's numerator"). The H1c verdict does not depend on it.
- Why daily transactions fell by most of their level between 2026-03-31 and 2026-04-02. BigQuery shows who stopped (Sweat Economy's `.kaiching` signers, 98.7<!--n:usage_bq_step_kaiching_share_pct-->% of the step) but not why, and we found no published account (`docs/usage.md`, exploratory).
- Partner and channel numbers rest on one key-gated source, the Explorer API. The repository commits its daily aggregates and a sample of swaps, not every swap.

## Optional: needs the network, a key or gcloud

Nothing here is needed to check the results. These commands refresh or re-check the committed snapshots against the live sources. Run them in a throwaway clone. Several overwrite files in `data/raw/` and `data/MANIFEST.json`. The two no-key on-chain re-checks (`fetch_escrow verify --sample 60` and `fetch_fee_evidence trace`) reproduce the committed data byte for byte, but each still rewrites its entry in `data/MANIFEST.json` (a new `fetched_at` and a different `source` text). The dashboard's data fingerprint is the hash of that file, so afterwards `python3 -m pipeline.render --check` and two unit tests (`test_render_check_passes`, `test_committed_site_is_current`) fail until you run `python3 -m pipeline.render`; `check --manifest` and `check --numbers` still pass, and `git checkout -- .` undoes it. A refreshed snapshot can also change a number that the prose quotes, which `python3 -m pipeline.check --numbers` would then report.

Eleven committed inputs have no command in this repository that regenerates them, so no command below refreshes them: the per-delivery table behind the ladder (`data/raw/escrow/deliveries.csv`; `fetch_escrow` re-checks its rows against the chain but never writes it), the exchange-label list and the Binance snapshot behind it (`data/raw/labels/`, two files), four small RPC files in `data/raw/rpc/` (`buyback_balance_endpoints.json`, `pool_endpoints_archival.json` and `rating_assumptions.json` feed H1a, H1c and the rating rule; `buyback_wnear_endpoints.json` is hashed like the rest but read by no code here) and the four files in `data/raw/unstakers/`. ## Integrity rules

- **The pre-registration is frozen.** `PREREG.md` sits at git tag `prereg-freeze` (commit `413d776`) with SHA-256 `19e470be5398f67ce7b6b5a51f007c0c7a0fcc059b61853c52caf7401452e240`. It is never edited. Step 7 checks all three. The tag and the commit have the same timestamp, so the tag adds no independent evidence of timing.
- **The addendum is a dated record, and append-only from 2026-11-08.** `PREREG-ADDENDUM.md` records, as dated entries, the post-freeze results, method choices and corrections for the pre-registered work, including how its frozen definitions were read and implemented. `PREREG.md` itself is never edited. The addendum was described as append-only before 2026-11-08, but five commits (`e0edac8`, `8e59a3f`, `5188f5a`, `01187d5`, `15f2859`) changed nine existing lines; its 2026-11-08 entry lists them, and from that entry on the text above it is pinned by SHA-256 in `tests/test_integrity.py`, so an edit of it fails the tests. Corrections made in other files before 2026-11-08 are listed in the same entry where git shows them; the full record of every change is `git log`.
- **Numbers in prose are machine-checked, but only where they are marked.** A number that exists in `data/metrics.json` carries a `<!--n:…-->` marker, and `python3 -m pipeline.check --numbers` fails if the marked number differs from the metric (step 3). It reads `README.md`, `THESIS.md`, `PREREG-ADDENDUM.md` and `docs/*.md`. It cannot find a metric number that has no marker, so keeping the markers complete is the writers' job: mark every figure you take from `data/metrics.json`. The dashboard's numbers are tied to their sources by `tests/test_render.py`.
- **Raw snapshots are hash-checked.** `data/MANIFEST.json` records the SHA-256 of every file in `data/raw/`, and `python3 -m pipeline.check --manifest` fails on any mismatch, missing file or unlisted file (step 1). Eleven inputs have no regenerating command here (see the optional table).
- **Pre-registered and exploratory results are labelled.** The ladder is exploratory (chosen after the freeze) and does not feed the rating rule. It was developed alongside the pre-registered tests, not after them: a first split of the buyback wallet's inflows into collectors and single-use accounts was committed in `33d7ec7` (2026-11-06 03:00:11 UTC), its code and four of its five metrics in `51e396b` (2026-11-06 23:19:20 UTC), before `1c032a0` (the H1b and H1c results, 23:23:40) and `9ee9082` (H1a as written, 23:49:26), and `THESIS.md` was first committed with the ladder as its headline in `3bf67c3` (2026-11-07 00:21:24 UTC). The addendum records this (entry 2026-11-08, which qualifies its 2026-11-07 00:21 UTC entry), and `THESIS.md` labels the ladder wherever it appears. `THESIS.md` says at the top that only the hypothesis tests, the rating rule and the dated predictions are pre-registered, and its section 4 labels its exploratory additions where they appear.
- **Forward tests are scored by a script that refuses a live run before 2026-11-14 00:00 UTC.** `pipeline/score_forward.py` scores F1-F4 as `PREREG.md` words them, with the operational readings listed in its docstring and in the addendum (2026-11-08 02:03 UTC entry, item 7). Its `--from-snapshot` mode re-scores a saved snapshot offline and does not compare the clock with the cutoff, and it believes the snapshot's own recorded times (see the optional table), so a score file is evidence together with its snapshot and the git history. The script was written after the forward window opened, which the addendum discloses. Its first dry run read no scoring-window day for F2 to F4 and only the window-opening block for F1 (item 7 of the 2026-11-06 23:49 UTC entry); later dry runs, and direct reads of the buyback wallet's balance, did touch window days and are disclosed in the 2026-11-08 05:04 UTC entry. A test with missing data is `not scorable`, never estimated. Every result is published, misses included. Output of its `--dry-run` mode is never a score.
- **What these checks do not show.** Git timestamps are written by the committing machine, and so are the times in `data/MANIFEST.json`; apart from the GitHub record below, nothing external attests either. `PREREG.md` says its hash "is published on NEAR mainnet (see `docs/ANCHOR.md` once anchored)"; no `docs/ANCHOR.md` is committed, so there is no on-chain anchor to check here. BigQuery job IDs, which embed their creation time, are recorded only for the H1b/H1c runs and the October escrow scan. The H1a queries have none, so for H1a the order of events rests on commit and manifest times alone. One external record does exist: GitHub's activity list dates the push of the freeze commit to 2026-11-05 22:53:33 UTC (`PREREG-ADDENDUM.md`, 2026-11-12 23:50 UTC entry, item 2).
